This method advances beyond the [diagonal coefficient loader](gibbs-preparation.md): the Hamiltonian has noncommuting terms, and the initial reduced system is maximally mixed. No eigenvectors or dense exponential produce a quantum gate. A dense answer serves only as a tiny independent check. Truncated-Taylor LCU and Gibbs-preparation algorithms provide the broader access/normalization context {cite:p}`sim_taylor_lcu_2015,sim_gibbs_poulin_wocjan_2009,sim_gibbs_chowdhury_somma_2017`.

## Model and thermal task

The example uses one system qubit and

$$H/E_0=0.6Z+0.4X,\qquad E_0=1,\qquad k_B=1,\qquad\beta=1.25/E_0.$$

Temperature is $T=0.8E_0$. Since X and Z do not commute, a Z-basis bitstring does not give the energy. The target is $\rho_\beta=e^{-\beta H}/\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-\beta H})$, or the separately stated thermal means of X, Z and H.

Prepare a Bell pair with an H gate and a CX gate:

$$|\Phi_2\rangle=(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2.$$

More generally $|\Phi_d\rangle=d^{-1/2}\sum_x|x\rangle_S|x\rangle_R$ has reduced system state $I/d$. System wires come first, equally sized reference wires next, and the LCU label register last. The default circuit uses one system, one reference and two label qubits. Input and label preparation are actual gates from all-zero wires, with fresh reset instructions in each exported measured attempt.

## Imaginary-time polynomial and explicit access

For degree K, approximate the filter by

$$T_K(H)=\sum_{k=0}^K\frac{(-\beta H/2)^k}{k!}=\sum_j b_jP_j,\qquad s=\sum_j|b_j|.$$

The coefficients are generated by Pauli multiplication, not matrix exponentiation or classical diagonalization. Each Hermitian power uses $(HP+PH)/2=HP$ because P is a polynomial in H. This lets anticommuting Pauli pairs cancel algebraically before floating arithmetic. No tolerance pruning is used. Aggregation still may grow exponentially, and it is not the scalable word-series oracle.

At $K=6$, the default aggregate contains I, X and Z, with $s\approx1.7496670$. PREPARE loads amplitudes $\sqrt{|b_j|/s}$ by controlled RY gates. Indexed Pauli operations and coefficient signs implement SELECT; inverse PREPARE is included. Padded labels act as identity. The actual access circuit has zero-label block $T_K/s$, which tests compare with an independent matrix Taylor recurrence. Every controlled access operation is lowered before export/counting.

Applying this access to the Bell input and accepting only zero labels gives a conditional purification. Tracing out the reference without conditioning gives

$$\rho_K=\frac{T_KT_K^\dagger}{\operatorname{Tr}(T_K^\dagger T_K)},\qquad
p_K=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(T_K^\dagger T_K)}{d s^2}.$$

The reference marginal is $(T_K^\dagger T_K)^T/\operatorname{Tr}(T_K^\dagger T_K)$; it must not be confused with the system output. Reference measurements conditioned on a result would change the state task. The full measured-trial circuit instead resets the reference without conditioning and then reads the system in the requested Pauli basis.

At $\beta=1.25/E_0$ and $K=6$, actual access-block spectral error is about $1.82\times10^{-16}$ against the polynomial, while polynomial-filter error against $e^{-\beta H/2}$ is about $7.94\times10^{-7}$. The reduced-state Gibbs trace distance is about $3.93\times10^{-7}$, with unit trace and a positive minimum eigenvalue. These are different metrics: correct access to a polynomial does not make the polynomial exact.

The conditional circuit means are approximately $\langle Z\rangle=-0.5965569$, $\langle X\rangle=-0.3977046$ and $\langle H\rangle/E_0=-0.5170160$. Exact thermal values are about $-0.5965576$, $-0.3977050$ and $-0.5170165$. At degree 8 the measured trace-distance discrepancy drops to about $1.11\times10^{-9}$. Saved checks are ideal numerical evidence, not hardware results or formal interval certificates.

## Accepted measured trials and oracle costs

Default ideal acceptance is about $p_K=0.4685902$. Eight complete attempts suffice for at least one accepted measured output with 99% ideal acceptance confidence. The attempts include fresh system/reference/label resets, Bell preparation, actual access/uncomputation, label measurement, unconditioned reference reset, basis changes and system readout. Every branch runs the whole schedule; failed measured trials are discarded afterward. No early-abort saving or amplitude amplification is claimed.

The default Z attempt lowers to 21 CX, 8 RZ, 7 RY, 6 X, 6 T, 6 T-dagger and 2 H gates, plus 5 resets and 3 measurements. The X attempt has its actual final H-basis change and different lowered counts. These original logical exports retain unsynthesized continuous-angle rotations. Native T/T-dagger instructions are counted separately from arbitrary rotations; their presence does not certify the rest of the circuit.

Energy estimation uses independent X and Z groups, $\widehat E=0.6\widehat Z+0.4\widehat X$. A 0.03 statistical energy-error allocation assigns 0.03 error to each Pauli mean because $0.6+0.4=1$. ## Conditioning, synthesis and physical faults

Postselection can amplify coherent error. Suppose the coherent Bell+LCU unitary had a certified operator-norm error $\eta$, and its ideal accepted-branch norm were $a=\sqrt{p_K}$. For $\eta<a$, conservative bounds are

$$D(\widetilde\rho_K,\rho_K)\leq\min(1,2\eta/a),\qquad
|\Delta\langle P\rangle|\leq\min(2,4\eta/a),\qquad
\widetilde p_K\geq(a-\eta)^2.$$

Reference discard/reset without conditioning cannot increase trace distance. This norm bound covers the coherent unitary; irreversible initialization, reset/readout faults and statistical noise have separate contracts. With a hypothetical $\eta=10^{-4}$, the reduced-state bound is about $0.0002922$, Pauli-mean bias about $0.0005843$, and acceptance lower bound about $0.4684533$. The ledger separately computes a retry proposal from that lower bound. **Certified synthesis is not implemented:** a QDK mean-fit scalar is not such an operator-norm certificate.

Thermal polynomial error, coherent synthesis conditioning, sampling failure, acceptance failure and physical faults are separated. The original 742,750-attempt logical proposal assigns 0.005 to physical failure without a physical projection or combined success certificate. Its logical counts omit routing, factories and physical reset/discard/readout timing. The conditional resource study below has its own compiled circuits, workloads and allocations. Classical aggregation/materialization time is recorded independently; the frozen Grover baseline is unchanged.

### Conditional physical-resource study

The [complete-instrument study](../../assets/generated/instrument-resources/README.md) computes damping-Z, Lindblad-Z and the two thermal Pauli groups: four measured tasks, eight physical profiles and 23 retained qubit/time Pareto points. The thermal resource task fixes $H/E_0=0.6Z+0.4X$, $\beta=1.25/E_0$ and degree 6, with **unamplified** Bell preparation and explicit LCU access. The [X report](../../assets/generated/instrument-resources/thermal-X/report.json) and [Z report](../../assets/generated/instrument-resources/thermal-Z/report.json) include every reset, label acceptance measurement, unconditional reference discard and system readout. They do not cost the separate known-input amplification method.

Actual Ross–Selinger Clifford+T sequences are generated with Qiskit 2.6.2 {cite:p}`resource_ross_selinger_2014,resource_qiskit_262,resource_rsgridsynth_022`. Both compiled measured attempts contain 382 T/T-dagger gates, 36 CX, five resets and three measurements. Numerical per-gate Frobenius errors plus guards give $\eta\approx5.10\times10^{-6}$. The study evaluates source/routed/synthesized instruments and applies the guarded numerical proposals $\widetilde p_K\geq(\sqrt{p_K}-\eta)^2\approx0.4685833$ and mean bias $4\eta/\sqrt{p_K}\approx2.98\times10^{-5}$. A mean-fit T-count proxy would not certify these conditional quantities. Neither the numerical guard nor the small checks are formal interval or combined confidence certificates.

The resource study gives **each Pauli group** failure budgets 0.00125 for sampling, acceptance, physical faults and factory availability. With statistical error 0.03 per mean, it requires **16,396 accepted-shot slots per group**, each capped at **26 complete attempts**: 426,296 attempts per group and **852,592 total attempts**. The two groups' budget sum is 0.01, and their weighted numerical energy-error proposal is approximately 0.0300304. The original 14,855-slots/group, 25-retry, 742,750-attempt logical proposal above retains its original allocations. None of these large shot ensembles has been executed.
