## Motivation and intuition

Generalized quantum signal processing (GQSP) interleaves general single-qubit
rotations with a unitary signal controlled by one ancilla. Interference creates
a complex polynomial of the signal, including both even and odd powers.
Motlagh and Wiebe establish the polynomial-pair characterization for this
mechanism {cite:p}`ham_gqsp_2024`. Our first circuit supplies the angles and
constructs the resulting polynomial; it does not solve the inverse problem
of finding angles for a requested function.

## Fundamental algorithm and declared convention

The explicit signal is

$$W_n=e^{i\chi}\left(\prod_{j=0}^{n-2}\mathrm{CZ}_{j,j+1}\right)
\left(\bigotimes_{j=0}^{n-1}R_Z(2z_j)R_Y(2y_j)\right).$$

Every data wire has actual rotations. The entangling layer has actual gates;
no dense matrix or symbolic oracle is appended. This family is a unitary
signal example, with its own size parameter. It is not an implementation of
the evolution of an arbitrary specified Hamiltonian.

Ancilla q0 is the least-significant wire, and data wire j occupies q(j+1).
The signal operation is

$$D(W)=|0\rangle\langle0|\otimes W+|1\rangle\langle1|\otimes I.$$

The implementation uses the determinant-one Euler convention

$$E_j=R_Z(\phi_j)R_Y(2\theta_j)R_Z(\lambda_j).$$

Chronological gates are $E_0,D,E_1,\ldots,D,E_d$, hence
$V=E_dD\cdots E_1DE_0$. For
$E_j=\begin{pmatrix}a_j&b_j\\c_j&d_j\end{pmatrix}$, ascending-power
coefficient vectors obey

$$P_0=a_0,\quad Q_0=c_0,\qquad
P_j(z)=a_jzP_{j-1}(z)+b_jQ_{j-1}(z),\quad
Q_j(z)=c_jzP_{j-1}(z)+d_jQ_{j-1}(z).$$

The source therefore constructs first-column blocks $P(W),Q(W)$ of degree
at most d. Unitarity gives the ideal real-parameter identity
$|P(z)|^2+|Q(z)|^2=1$ on $|z|=1$. The numerical checks evaluate selected
points and do not constitute a uniform floating-point certificate.

The paper's arXiv v2 Equation 6 uses a different phase/sign gauge. If that
matrix is denoted $R_p$, direct entry comparison gives

$$E(\theta,\phi,\lambda)
=e^{i((\phi+\lambda)/2+\pi)}
R_p(\theta,-\phi,-\lambda-\pi).$$

This explicit factor matters to complex amplitudes. The implementation retains
its own Euler convention and forward recurrence throughout; it does not
silently substitute the paper's angle arrays.

## Small circuit and independent checks

Choose $W=e^{i\pi/4}R_Z(\pi/2)$, whose eigenvalues are 1 and i, and

$$E_0=(\theta,\phi,\lambda)=(\pi/4,0,0),\qquad
E_1=(\pi/4,0,\pi/2).$$

Direct two-by-two algebra gives

$$P(z)=\tfrac12(e^{-i\pi/4}z-e^{i\pi/4}),\qquad
Q(z)=\tfrac12(e^{-i\pi/4}z+e^{i\pi/4}).$$

Both powers have complex coefficients. The implemented input is
$(|0\rangle+i|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$, prepared with H and S. In `(data,ancilla)`
order the analytic joint probabilities are $(1/4,1/4,0,1/2)$ for
$(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)$. At eigenvalue i, swapping the control changes acceptance from 0
to 1; reversing the rotations changes it to 1/2. Omitting $\chi$ changes
the signal and its polynomial response.

## End-to-end resources and fault-tolerant analysis

A zero-controlled signal is built from two X wrappers, controlled rotations,
compiled three-wire CCZ phases and a P($\chi$) on the control inside the
wrapper. The signal's global phase becomes a conditional phase. Ordinary
signal exports use a literal phase-identity gate sequence, so the source
and exported complete trials have zero omitted overall phase.

In this unoptimized source construction, one controlled signal has
$25n-14$ named primitive instructions: four operations for each controlled
Y/Z rotation, 17 for each CCZ, two X wrappers and one conditional P.
The d-signal body therefore has

$$G_{\rm body}=d(25n-14)+3(d+1),\qquad Q=n+1.$$

Input preparation adds 0, 1, 2 or 3 gates for the supported basis or
H/S/X inputs. A complete measured trial also resets and measures all n+1
wires. The saved complete circuits match these source expressions. They do not
count physical cycles or synthesized T sequences for
the parameterized rotations. The literal CCZ constructions separately use
seven T/Tdag instructions per edge and signal call.

The original suite has these complete trial sizes. Every row includes the
implemented input preparation, all n+1 resets, the entire coherent schedule
and all n+1 final measurements.

Ancilla outcome zero accepts. An accepted sample has its state-dependent
probability $\|P(W)|\psi\rangle\|^2$; every failed attempt still runs the
complete reset, preparation, signal, rotation and readout schedule. General
sampling confidence and a certified acceptance lower bound are unimplemented.
The suite compares ideal probabilities, without executing a finite-shot
population. Rotation synthesis, physical faults, factory/routing/controller
costs and final classical-statistical latency remain uncomputed. A future
surface-code comparison needs a matched output, error and success contract.

## Classical simulation, formalization and bounds

The source generator caps data wires at 64, degree at 64, angles at magnitude
32π and complete source size at 120,000 instructions. Dense checks are
separately limited to four total wires and 5,000 body instructions. The
suite's n=8 and n=64, d=6 exports are generation-only. A generated
large circuit would not establish its numerical or physical correctness.

Forward coefficient construction is bounded classical preprocessing. It
is neither an inverse phase compiler nor a proof kernel. The optional
companion notebook has five executed genuine code cells, including a
circuit drawing and independent small comparison. No Lean or Rocq proof
is reproduced. Qiskit 2.6.2 supplies gate and simulator infrastructure
under Apache-2.0 {cite:p}`sim_qiskit_262`; this prototype's construction and references are original
MIT source. The existing lock remains unchanged.

The [37 original test outcomes](../../assets/generated/simulation/gqsp/test-case-outcomes.json)
and [public evidence summary](../../assets/generated/simulation/gqsp/checks-summary.json)
retain the validation scope. Tests and the suite passed in an earlier full
run that then failed during notebook readiness/process observation. A
separate unchanged-source notebook-only continuation completed the five
cells. These events remain distinct; the original failed run is not relabeled
as a successful full workflow.

## Reproduce and download

Download the [flat original-code package](../../assets/generated/simulation/gqsp-reproduce.zip),
or keep the [original generator](../../assets/generated/simulation/gqsp/generate_simulation_gqsp.py),
[module](../../assets/generated/simulation/gqsp/gqsp.py),
[environment lock](../../assets/generated/simulation/gqsp/requirements-lock.txt) and
[MIT license](../../assets/generated/simulation/gqsp/LICENSE.txt) beside the
[original executed notebook](../../assets/generated/simulation/gqsp/simulation_gqsp.ipynb).
Start Jupyter in that folder. The generator selects its explicit sibling
module and needs no private repository or planning files in this layout.

```bash
python -m pip install -r requirements-lock.txt
python -B generate_simulation_gqsp.py --suite --output-dir ../gqsp-recomputed
python -B generate_simulation_gqsp.py --n 64 --degree 6 --no-sim \
  --output-dir ../gqsp-n64-recomputed
```

Choose fresh output directories outside the downloaded folder. Wider
requests require `--no-sim`; supplied angles, construction and simulation
caps remain explicit. The [saved-file checker](../../assets/generated/simulation/gqsp/verify_saved.py)
reads hashes and stored evidence without rerunning circuits. This public
folder's detached-mode portability check is separate from the completed
scientific checks and has not yet executed.
