Complete static and feedback QPE accounting

This executed companion reads the retained new synthesis and physical records. Native factory queries run in the bounded producer, once per task/variant. The target is the known phase .3 of P(2πφ), explicitly prepared with X. Fifteen fresh complete runs estimate a scalar median within 2^-m. The phase lies away from the branch cut for both m=3 and4. These are conditional engineering scenarios and ideal numerical checks, not hardware results or a formal confidence certificate.

Predicate decisions are charged even when false; no branch-specific machine or expected runtime saving is used. Both variants share the frozen physical task and model.

Startup workspace, explicit input preparation, actual controlled access, concrete synthesis, T injection/transport, factory retries, reset/readout and controller fault scenarios are included.
