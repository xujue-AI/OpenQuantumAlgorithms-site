Coupled Lindblad dynamics and grouped energy readout

The bounded density study uses two systems with H=0.6Z0Z1+0.35(X0+X1)H=0.6Z_0Z_1+0.35(X_0+X_1)H=0.6Z0​Z1​+0.35(X0​+X1​), local damping rates (0.8,0.5)(0.8,0.5)(0.8,0.5), t=1t=1t=1 and ℏ=1\hbar=1ℏ=1. The default input is ∣+i⟩|+i\rangle∣+i⟩ on system0 and ∣+⟩|+\rangle∣+⟩ on system1. A Bell input is also gate-prepared and checked. Each environment is reset before every local dilation; no outcomes are postselected.

The global positive-time palindrome includes all Hamiltonian components and local dissipators. Exact component gates do not make the combined dynamics exact: its splitting error is compared with an independently assembled, time-scaled 16×1616\times1616×16 Liouvillian exponential. A separate n-chain generator is executed for circuit counts; the full combined-generator accuracy study remains n=2.

Original MIT source/figures use Qiskit 2.6.2 (Apache-2.0), NumPy and SciPy 1.18.1 (BSD-3-Clause). No formal/Lean certificate or physical estimate is integrated.

Global second-order step¶

Chronological order: ZZ(dt/2), Xlayer(dt/2), D0(dt/2), D1(dt), D0(dt/2), Xlayer(dt/2), ZZ(dt/2). Every component receives total dt. A half damping step uses 1−e−γidt/21-e^{-\gamma_i dt/2}1−e−γi​dt/2. There is no separately approximated combined-H substep: the global error includes Hamiltonian-term and Hamiltonian/damping commutators.

System wires0/1 have environment wires2/3. D0 is called twice and D1 once per symmetric step, with resets before every call. The diagram shows one step plus final Z readout.

Full measured trials and energy groups¶

X/Y/Z readout rotates every system into its requested basis, measures all four wires and ignores both environment bits. The energy uses an X-group score 0.35(x0+x1)0.35(x_0+x_1)0.35(x0​+x1​) and a Z-group score 0.6z0z10.6z_0z_10.6z0​z1​. The two bits within a group may be correlated. One trial supplies one bounded scalar score; independent trials, not independent qubits, enter Hoeffding.

Executed circuit scaling and bounded density scope¶

The generator supports n up to16 with an open ZZ chain, an X layer and local damping. It prepares ∣+i⟩|+i\rangle∣+i⟩ on system0 and ∣+⟩|+\rangle∣+⟩ elsewhere. Its space is 2n logical wires. Counts below are actual circuit construction/decomposition at fixed r, not an equal-accuracy n comparison. Full GKSL error curves here are n=2; tests include one n=3 split-component functional check. Larger-n density accuracy and formal bounds remain unvalidated.

Scope and exclusions¶

Generation is capped at16 systems, 1,024 steps and 50,000 estimated/compiled primitive operations. Public density helpers remain two-system/four-wire checks, capped at128 steps; reference exposure is capped at100. The numerical energy-budget helper is n=2 and caps proposals at 1012 trials. General-n energy group widths grow with n, so fixed absolute statistical error requires an unexecuted O(n2/ϵstat2)O(n^2/\epsilon_{stat}^2)O(n2/ϵstat2​) conservative shot bound before choosing r(n) for systematic accuracy.

Physical reset/discard/readout times, routing, factories, code distance, coherent synthesis and classical coefficient/readout wall time are excluded. A static estimator cannot silently omit mid-circuit resets. No physical quantum advantage or integrated Lean proof is claimed.
