Quantum simulation: fault-tolerant analysis

The Hamiltonian-dynamics report contains implemented logical workloads for product formulas, Taylor-LCU attempts and QSP components. Their physical estimates remain pending. Open-system and Gibbs preparation reports are planned.

TrackRequired output contractCosts that must remain explicit
Hamiltonian dynamicsUnitary/state evolution or a measured observable at a stated timePreparation, access, approximation, synthesis, postselection/amplification, measurements and repetitions
Open systemsA specified channel/state or observableEnvironment/dilation preparation, reset/discard, channel approximation and measurement
Gibbs preparationA density operator, purification or thermal observableTemperature, Hamiltonian access, normalization/overlap assumptions, preparation and success costs

These are different tasks. A cosine filter is not full complex evolution, and a classical matrix exponential is a reference rather than a quantum preparation algorithm. Align the task and accuracy before comparing costs.

Use separate approximation, sampling and physical-fault budgets. The static arbitrary-rotation adapter supplies only conditional mean-fit projections and cannot currently cost dynamic measurement/reset instruments. The fault-tolerance appendix explains these boundaries.
