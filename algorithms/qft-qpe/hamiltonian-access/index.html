Phase estimation with Hamiltonian access

Motivation and intuition¶

An energy becomes a phase through Hamiltonian evolution. A useful QPE example must construct that controlled evolution and specify the input state, because the phase distribution depends on both Cleve et al., 1998Abrams & Lloyd, 1999. This companion replaces the known phase-gate example with an explicit noncommuting two-spin Hamiltonian. It also shows why keeping the target between measurements and preparing it afresh produce different joint records.

Checked scope

The saved study checks a full controlled three-wire kernel, two-bit/two-round QPE instruments and six fresh interference experiments. Its 15 tiny ideal circuit/instrument cases passed numerical reference checks. It supplies no ground-energy output or confidence experiment, compiled rotation sequence, physical estimate or formal proof. The model is analytically solvable and is a correctness example, with no application-advantage or chemical-accuracy claim.

Retained targets, feedback and fresh experiments¶

Let Pj(y)P_j(y)Pj​(y) be the two-bit QPE word law for eigencomponent j. Two rounds on one retained target have

J(y0,y1)=∑jwjPj(y0)Pj(y1).J(y_0,y_1)=\sum_j w_jP_j(y_0)P_j(y_1).J(y0​,y1​)=j∑​wj​Pj​(y0​)Pj​(y1​).
(4)

The latent eigenvalue is common to the rounds. Resetting the target between rounds instead gives Jfresh(y0,y1)=p(y0)p(y1)J_{\rm fresh}(y_0,y_1)=p(y_0)p(y_1)Jfresh​(y0​,y1​)=p(y0​)p(y1​) with p(y)=∑jwjPj(y)p(y)=\sum_jw_jP_j(y)p(y)=∑j​wj​Pj​(y). The saved target-reset negative exposes this difference; repeated rounds are not independent fresh draws from the marginal mixture.

The deferred six-wire circuit, four-wire clock-reuse circuit and three-wire feedback circuit implement the same retained law. Feedback measures U2U^2U2 first as the low bit, applies S†S^\daggerS† if that bit is one, and then measures the U1U^1U1 high bit Griffiths & Niu, 1996. History restarts for the next word; the data stay retained. All four joint outputs store 16 probabilities in order y0+4y1y_0+4y_1y0​+4y1​, where y0=c0+2c1y_0=c_0+2c_1y0​=c0​+2c1​ and y1=c2+2c3y_1=c_2+2c_3y1​=c2​+2c3​.

Fresh Hadamard experiments reset all three wires for each trial and measure cosine/sine fringes at powers 1, 2 and 4. Their signal is ⟨00∣Uk∣00⟩\langle00|U^k|00\rangle⟨00∣Uk∣00⟩, a spectral mixture. Its probability difference from the ground-component fringe is at most q. The six checked fringes fit this tiny-model contamination diagnostic; arbitrary mixed inputs need another certificate before using bounded-bias robust inference.
