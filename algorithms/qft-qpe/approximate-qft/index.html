Approximate Fourier transform

Motivation and construction¶

An exact mmm-wire Fourier circuit has quadratic controlled-phase count. Its longest-range phases are exponentially small. Omitting those phases reduces gate construction and eventual synthesis work at a declared coherent approximation cost, following the approximate-transform idea of Coppersmith Coppersmith, 1994.

build_qft(m, max_distance=b) retains the Hadamards and final swaps, and keeps only controlled phases with wire separation d≤bd\leq bd≤b. A retained gate has angle π/2d\pi/2^dπ/2d. The retained count is ∑d=1b(m−d)\sum_{d=1}^{b}(m-d)∑d=1b​(m−d), giving O(mb)O(mb)O(mb) controlled phases rather than O(m2)O(m^2)O(m2). Setting b=m-1 is the exact circuit; b=0 retains only Hadamards and swaps.

Approximation and scaling¶

For an omitted phase θ\thetaθ, ∥CP(θ)−I∥2=2∣sin⁡(θ/2)∣≤∣θ∣\|CP(\theta)-I\|_2=2|\sin(\theta/2)|\leq|\theta|∥CP(θ)−I∥2​=2∣sin(θ/2)∣≤∣θ∣. A telescoping product of unitary gates therefore gives the original bound

∥F~N−FN∥2≤min⁡ ⁣(2,∑d=b+1m−1(m−d)π2d)≤min⁡(2,πm/2b).\|\widetilde F_N-F_N\|_2\leq \min\!\left(2,\sum_{d=b+1}^{m-1}(m-d)\frac{\pi}{2^d}\right) \leq\min(2,\pi m/2^b).∥FN​−FN​∥2​≤min(2,d=b+1∑m−1​(m−d)2dπ​)≤min(2,πm/2b).
(1)

The inverse has the same bound. Choosing b≥⌈log⁡2(πm/η)⌉b\geq\lceil\log_2(\pi m/\eta)\rceilb≥⌈log2​(πm/η)⌉, capped at m−1m-1m−1, meets a sufficient spectral-norm omission allowance η\etaη. This bound covers the omitted ideal phases. It does not cover inaccuracies in retained rotations, physical faults or application readout confidence.

The generated table compares explicit source circuits. At m=32m=32m=32, cutoff b=14b=14b=14 retains 343 controlled phases instead of 496, with telescoping bound about 0.003067963. These larger circuits were generated and counted, not densely simulated. Logical count reduction alone does not establish physical improvement: retained rotation synthesis and factory scheduling must be compared under the same application and accuracy contract.

End-to-end resource analysis¶

A QFT-only unitary approximation does not establish confidence in an application output. This comparison uses a complete QPE task: prepare ∣1⟩|1\rangle∣1⟩, apply the actual phase gate U=P(2π×0.3)U=P(2\pi\times0.3)U=P(2π×0.3) through repeated controlled calls, apply exact or truncated inverse QFT, read the phase register and take the scalar median of fresh trials. The phase is known to the constructor, so this is a circuit/resource example rather than an application advantage.

One longest-distance controlled phase is omitted in each truncated circuit. Omission and the matched output contract¶

The spectral omission bound η\etaη implies output-distribution total variation at most min⁡(1,η)\min(1,\eta)min(1,η): pure-state trace distance is at most the state-vector norm difference, and measurement contracts distance. Omission therefore worsens a conservative single-trial bad-output bound. It is not an additive numerical phase bias and is not subtracted from ϵϕ\epsilon_\phiϵϕ​.

Using π<22/7\pi<22/7π<22/7, the ordinary two-adjacent-grid-point QPE theorem gives a conservative rational single-run bad bound 23/12123/12123/121. With a retained-rotation synthesis-TV allowance ϵs\epsilon_sϵs​, use

qbad≤23/121+ηTV+ϵs.q_{\rm bad}\le23/121+\eta_{\rm TV}+\epsilon_s.qbad​≤23/121+ηTV​+ϵs​.

Exact Fraction arithmetic chooses the least odd number R≤1001R\le1001R≤1001 of fresh complete trials whose binomial majority tail is at most 0.0049. A single-run bound at least one half or an insufficient cap returns an error instead of weakening the confidence target. The remaining whole-run physical-fault allowance is 0.005, leaving the configured total below 0.01.

The scalar median requires the phase to lie at least 2−m2^{-m}2−m from the [0,1)[0,1)[0,1) branch cut. Computed conditional physical result¶

The complete measured circuits use the same compilation settings and QDK adapter. Both physical profiles use 100 ns gates, 500 ns measurements, rotated surface code and round-based factories. The 0.005 whole-run physical allowance is divided across all fresh trials. Changing RRR can change the required data distance or factory allocation; that effect is included in each returned frontier.

The 24 bounded queries return 123 Pareto points. Omitting one gate saves five primitive operations per trial, but the conservative confidence contract increases repetitions from 15 to 127, 35 or 21. At each case’s fastest returned point, truncated/exact total serial-runtime ratios range from about 1.40 to 9.75. This grid therefore shows no total-runtime improvement under its declared omission/inference policy.

Observed output-TV losses at phase 0.3 are much smaller than the uniform bounds: approximately 0.00907, 0.00343 and 0.000463. Omission slightly improves this selected phase’s good-output mass at seven bits. These diagnostics do not choose the repetition policy. A sharper independently established application-specific omission or inference contract could change the comparison; no such improvement is validated here.

All Pareto points, code distances, factory choices, canonical counts and QASM/source hashes are retained. Peak qubits times total runtime is a rectangular space-time proxy. The QDK mean synthesis fit supplies no worst-case per-angle approximation or runtime-tail certificate. Physical initialization/reset, external loading and classical median/controller/decoder/verification latency are excluded. The existing Grover baseline remains unchanged.
