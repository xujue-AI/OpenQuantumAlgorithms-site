Iterative phase estimation with feedback

Motivation and fundamental version¶

The phase register can be measured one bit at a time when its inverse Fourier transform is immediately followed by readout. Earlier outcomes then select later single-qubit corrections, replacing coherent Fourier interactions with classical feedback Griffiths & Niu, 1996.

Our explicit circuit uses n+1n+1n+1 quantum wires and mmm classical output bits. In round r=0,…,m−1r=0,\ldots,m-1r=0,…,m−1 it resets the phase ancilla, applies H and 2m−r−12^{m-r-1}2m−r−1 controlled calls to UUU, applies corrections selected by previous recorded bits, applies H and measures into c[r]. A previous c[l]=1 triggers

P ⁣(−2π2r−l+1),l<r.P\!\left(-\frac{2\pi}{2^{r-l+1}}\right),\qquad l<r.P(−2r−l+12π​),l<r.
(1)

The least-significant output bit is obtained first. Displaying c[m-1]...c[0] gives the usual binary integer yyy and phase y/2my/2^my/2m. The eigenstate is prepared once and retained between rounds in the ideal instrument. For a superposition of eigenstates, the retained system and record become correlated; the checked final phase distribution is the expected mixture.

End-to-end space and time¶

The quantum width falls from n+mn+mn+m to n+1n+1n+1 while retaining mmm classical bits. Each complete execution still applies 2m−12^m-12m−1 controlled UUU calls, 2m2m2m H gates, mmm reset instructions, mmm measurements and up to m(m−1)/2m(m-1)/2m(m−1)/2 selected correction gates. Fifteen independent runs of the default use 465 controlled calls, 75 measurements and 75 resets, with fresh preparation for each run.

The source ledger separates unconditional primitive operations from conditional blocks. A conditional gate is charged on the corresponding branch; recording a worst-case branch bound does not produce an average latency model. The executed circuit includes reset even though an ideal measured eigenstate can allow equivalent feedback initialization strategies. Reset faults and classical feed-forward delays are not silently omitted.

Matched complete task and common feedback schedule¶

The comparison fixes U=P(2π×0.3)U=P(2\pi\times0.3)U=P(2π×0.3), an X-prepared ∣1⟩|1\rangle∣1⟩ target, and m=3m=3m=3 or 4. Each variant repeats its entire initialized, prepared and measured circuit 15 times, then returns the scalar median of y/2my/2^my/2m, with absolute phase error target 2−m2^{-m}2−m. The promise 2−m≤ϕ≤1−2−m2^{-m}\le\phi\le1-2^{-m}2−m≤ϕ≤1−2−m keeps the good interval away from the branch cut. The usual ideal single-run probability bound 8/π28/\pi^28/π2 and its 15-run majority tail are separate from synthesis and physical faults Cleve et al., 1998Griffiths & Niu, 1996.

Both variants explicitly reset every input wire before preparation and retain all 2m−12^m-12m−1 complete controlled calls. Feedback also retains all mmm round resets, mmm readouts and m(m−1)/2m(m-1)/2m(m−1)/2 predicates. The target remains protected during reset, readout, feedback, factory supply and routing time. Startup workspace is initialized for every complete run.

The compiler gives each local conditional correction an independent slot, generates an actual ancilla-free Ross–Selinger Clifford+T sequence and reconstructs its original if_else. It preserves the fixed two-wire layout and every reset/readout boundary. The executable feedback circuit is distinct from the separately labelled all-corrections accounting envelope. This envelope charges every possible correction and provisions one fixed code distance, factory configuration and retry cap before any result is observed. False predicates still consume a controller decision, and skipped correction slots retain their full reserved idle time. No branch chooses another machine or supplies a lower expected-runtime claim Ross & Selinger, 2014.

The physical scenario uses the existing serialized surface-code instrument model: physical error 10-4, 100 ns gates and initialization, 500 ns measurements, 1,000 ns code cycles and 10,000 ns controller reactions. Concrete synthesis receives a guarded numerical operator allowance 2×10−52\times10^{-5}2×10−5 per complete run. Physical faults and capped factory availability each receive a separate 0.0025 allowance over all 15 runs. The recorded ideal majority tail, 15 times the synthesis allowance, and these two fault allowances sum below 0.01 under the stated assumptions Litinski, 2019Horsman et al., 2012Microsoft, n.d..

Neighbor routing, protected parity slots, magic-state transport/injection, initialization, reset, measurement, every controller decision and capped complete factory retries are charged. Early factory success injects immediately and pads the remaining supply slot afterward, rather than holding a magic state for an uncharged interval. Factory output quality and independent pipeline availability are distinct terms. The code-distance domain is odd distances 3 through 15, with one just-in-time factory.

Classical decoding is included once after all runs. It stores 15m15m15m output bits and applies a fixed 105-comparator network: 15m15m15m bit-decoding slots, 105(m+3)105(m+3)105(m+3) comparison/write slots and one median-selection slot. Each slot uses the declared controller reaction and fault scenario; all reserved patches remain charged during this pass. These are controller-ISA scenario costs, not calibrated hardware latencies. The model assumes classical record storage and Boolean logic reliability are covered by its per-decision controller scenario. Separate idle classical-memory calibration and controller transistor area are unavailable.

Executed comparison and limits¶

The original source instruments agree with the independent Fourier law within 1.8×10−151.8\times10^{-15}1.8×10−15. Actual synthesized feedback differs from its ideal source distribution by total variation 1.20×10−71.20\times10^{-7}1.20×10−7 at three bits and 7.20×10−77.20\times10^{-7}7.20×10−7 at four bits, below their respective guarded numerical allocations. Removing the conditional corrections changes a probability by about 0.373 and 0.691, demonstrating that the branch behavior is exercised.

Conditional engineering comparison

Guarded floating matrix checks and small instrument enumeration are numerical evidence, not a formal synthesis interval or hardware confidence certificate. Encoder, readout, controller and code-exposure fits are declared scenarios. Tracked Pauli bases, lattice geometry, decoder throughput and factory/data handoff remain conditional assumptions. Offline compilation and presentation-only final reporting time are excluded. Physical qubit counts cover quantum patches and factories. This separate comparison preserves all earlier static QPE projections and the frozen Grover baseline.

The tested feedback distributions cover grid and off-grid phases, all tested output-bit sizes through five, global phases, a Y eigenstate and non-eigenstate mixtures. Primitive lowering preserves tested instrument probabilities. Independent review additionally checked entangled targets and conjugated multi-qubit eigenstates. The instrument checker is capped at eight quantum/classical bits, 4,096 top-level operations and 1,024 branches. It drops numerically zero branches below 10-15 and checks remaining total probability. These are bounded ideal numerical checks, not a physical simulator or a Lean theorem.

The scalar-median confidence contract and its phase branch-cut assumption are defined in the parent page. No noisy-hardware inference or circular median is claimed.
