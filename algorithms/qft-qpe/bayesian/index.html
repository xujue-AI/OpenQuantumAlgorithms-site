Bayesian phase estimation on a circular grid

Motivation and intuition¶

An individual fringe cannot distinguish all phase aliases. Bayesian inference keeps the alternatives as a probability distribution, updates it after a measurement, and uses that distribution to choose another experiment. Published Bayesian methods motivate this route Wiebe & Granade, 2016Berg, 2021.

Fundamental method and its limits¶

The model has one fixed unknown eigenphase. For a fresh eigenstate satisfying U∣ψ⟩=e2πiϕ∣ψ⟩U|\psi\rangle=e^{2\pi i\phi}|\psi\rangleU∣ψ⟩=e2πiϕ∣ψ⟩, a controlled-UkU^kUk experiment and control phase −β-\beta−β give

p0(ϕ∣k,β)=12[1+cos⁡(2πkϕ−β)].p_0(\phi\mid k,\beta)=\tfrac12[1+\cos(2\pi k\phi-\beta)].p0​(ϕ∣k,β)=21​[1+cos(2πkϕ−β)].
(1)

Represent the prior by nonnegative weights wiw_iwi​ at the dyadic atoms ϕi=i/G\phi_i=i/Gϕi​=i/G. After outcome bbb, multiply by pb(ϕi)p_b(\phi_i)pb​(ϕi​) and normalize. An observed outcome with zero model evidence reports a contradiction; the implementation does not reset the prior or add probability support. Unsupported positive-support underflow is rejected.

Our original fixed-length greedy policy evaluates powers 1,2,4,8,16,321,2,4,8,16,321,2,4,8,16,32 and offsets 0,π/2,π,3π/20,\pi/2,\pi,3\pi/20,π/2,π,3π/2. It scans candidates in the supplied order and replaces its current choice only when the expected Shannon-entropy reduction improves by more than 10-12 nats. It receives no true phase. Finite-grid entropy can favor different powers than a continuous prior; the rule is not a resource-optimal or noise-robust theorem. An individual observation can increase entropy or widen the posterior.

The output contains a MAP atom, a circular mean when its resultant is not numerically ambiguous, and a shortest contiguous credible grid-cell arc. Half-cell endpoints give a minimum displayed width 1/G1/G1/G. Such an arc can span low-density regions in a multimodal posterior and is not a disjoint HPD set. A posterior mass of 95% is not a uniform frequentist coverage guarantee, a certified continuous-phase error interval or a hardware success bound.

Important variants¶

This explicit grid method is an original bounded example, not a reproduction of every procedure in the Bayesian literature. Wiebe--Granade’s efficient inference and the mixed-prior representation are separate method references Wiebe & Granade, 2016Berg, 2021. Multimodal uncertainty over a single fixed phase is different from a freshly prepared state with several spectral components. Our single-phase likelihood does not support the latter. Likelihood calibration, decoherence-aware experiment choice, continuous representations and adaptive stopping require separate contracts. Phayes remains an inspected external package; it is not used by this implementation.

Small circuit and prescribed study¶

The pedagogical family is Un=⨂j=1nP(θ)U_n=\bigotimes_{j=1}^{n}P(\theta)Un​=⨂j=1n​P(θ) and ∣ψ⟩=∣1⟩⊗n|\psi\rangle=|1\rangle^{\otimes n}∣ψ⟩=∣1⟩⊗n, with θ=(2πϕnominal)/n\theta=(2\pi\phi_{\rm nominal})/nθ=(2πϕnominal​)/n stored as binary64. The stored-angle eigenphase and nominal phase are reported separately. This classically easy family supplies a transparent correctness example, not application advantage.

Every controlled base call uses three P gates and two CX gates, repeated literally kkk times. Each trial resets all n+1n+1n+1 wires, performs the nnn X preparation gates, applies the control phase and reads the control. No P(kθ)P(k\theta)P(kθ) shortcut, opaque matrix gate or free eigenstate oracle is used. The diagram has two literal calls.

End-to-end resource analysis¶

For a complete recorded transcript with NNN observations and powers kjk_jkj​, let K=∑jkjK=\sum_jk_jK=∑j​kj​. This implementation has n+1n+1n+1 quantum wires and charges

N fresh preparations/readouts,K controlled base calls.N\ \text{fresh preparations/readouts},\qquad K\ \text{controlled base calls}.N fresh preparations/readouts,K controlled base calls.
(2)

The raw source totals are 3nK+N3nK+N3nK+N P, 2nK2nK2nK CX, 2N2N2N H, nNnNnN X, (n+1)N(n+1)N(n+1)N resets and NNN measurements. The P offset is emitted and counted even at zero angle. These are unsynthesized source counts; Clifford-angle P gates are not all T gates. The full source-operation total is 5nK+(2n+5)N5nK+(2n+5)N5nK+(2n+5)N.

The policy makes N+1N+1N+1 evaluations, including initial validation, and NNN Bayes updates. For CCC candidates these require O(NGC)O(NGC)O(NGC) numerical work. The final credible-cell scan is explicitly bounded at O(G2)O(G^2)O(G2); its CPU time is recorded separately. The online posterior uses O(G)O(G)O(G) storage; retaining all demonstration posterior histories uses O(NG)O(NG)O(NG) storage. The structural quantum envelope is K≤Nkmax⁡K\le Nk_{\max}K≤Nkmax​ and 5nNkmax⁡+(2n+5)N5nNk_{\max}+(2n+5)N5nNkmax​+(2n+5)N source operations. This is a construction bound, not a phase-precision or predictive hardware-confidence guarantee.

Physical reset and controller implementation, all policy processing latency, idle exposure, rotation synthesis and its certificate, surface-code distance, factory supply and physical faults remain uncomputed. The shared static estimator rejects reset/dynamic schedules; dropping resets would change this contract. A future physical instrument record must retain the complete transcript or declared envelope and its classical pauses. No improvement to the frozen surface-code baseline is claimed.

Verification, classical simulation and formalization¶

Executed checks compare complete small circuit operators and probabilities with an independent diagonal-power matrix, and recursive posteriors with a direct scalar product of likelihoods. Tests cover circular wrap, opposite modes, normalized weights, impossible observations, bounded types and literal preparation/access/readout. The SDK Born normalization retains the raw norm and rejects a deviation above 2×10−112\times10^{-11}2×10−11; a normalized floating result is not a formal accuracy certificate. Across the prescribed study, the maximum full-operator residual is 1.27×10−151.27\times10^{-15}1.27×10−15, the source-to-lowered operator residual is 5.20×10−155.20\times10^{-15}5.20×10−15, and the scalar probability residual is 2.73×10−152.73\times10^{-15}2.73×10−15. Recursive posteriors agree with the independent batch reference within 2.50×10−162.50\times10^{-16}2.50×10−16.

The complete test file passed 99 cases without skips or deselection. A separate 48-observation replay from the four exported source/lock/license files matched the first study record in every scientific field, excluding only timing. The real static notebook executed all five code cells with no new draws. These execution checks are distinct from the earlier source review.

The simulator is separately capped at three target wires. Source construction allows 32 target wires and at most 128 literal calls per circuit, with a 200,000-operation circuit cap. Full transcripts stop at 128 observations and 1,000,000 operations; grid sizes are powers of two from 16 to 4096 with G≥4kmax⁡G\ge4k_{\max}G≥4kmax​. This resolution rule is not a quadrature certificate. The producer has a 120-second/16-MiB export envelope and separate larger source-only mode. Generated-only sizes carry no numerical validation or posterior result: the executed generator produced an eight-target/nine-total-wire companion with 1,301 source operations and a 32-target/33-total-wire circuit with 20,549 source operations. Neither larger size was densely simulated. No Lean/Rocq proof or Python-to-proof correspondence runs here; the QPE chapter retains its external proof references and their limits.
