Fourier transform and phase estimation

Motivation and intuition¶

Many quantum tasks encode the answer in an eigenphase: an energy in Hamiltonian evolution, an order in modular arithmetic, or a probability in amplitude amplification. A phase is not directly visible in a computational-basis measurement. Phase estimation uses controlled evolution and interference to turn it into a classical binary record Cleve et al., 1998.

The quantum Fourier transform (QFT) combines the phases carried by a register. Phase estimation first prepares a superposition of different evolution lengths, then applies an inverse QFT so that matching phases add and mismatching phases cancel. QFT produces a quantum state; it does not return every entry of a classical Fourier transform in one measurement. This chapter supplies reusable components for the survey’s applications Dalzell et al., 2025.

Implemented scope

Exact and truncated QFT, textbook QPE and one-ancilla feedback QPE have explicit circuits and independent small-instance checks. Five static-QPE surface-code projections are computed under the audited conditional mean-fit model. Eigenstate preparation for an arbitrary application and certified rotation synthesis remain application responsibilities. No integrated Lean proof is supplied.

The circuit uses mmm Hadamards, m(m−1)/2m(m-1)/2m(m−1)/2 controlled-phase gates and ⌊m/2⌋\lfloor m/2\rfloor⌊m/2⌋ swaps. Wire q[0] is the least-significant bit. Swaps are included, so the matrix is the expression above rather than a bit-reversed variant. The inverse reverses the gate order and the phase signs.

QPE accepts an explicit nnn-qubit circuit UUU and an explicit preparation circuit for an eigenstate

It prepares mmm phase qubits with Hadamards, applies controlled U2jU^{2^j}U2j from phase wire jjj, applies FN†F_N^\daggerFN†​ and measures those mmm wires. The decoded integer yyy estimates ϕ\phiϕ by y/Ny/Ny/N. Our generator constructs each power from 2j2^j2j complete controlled calls to the supplied UUU; no power or dense matrix is treated as a free oracle. A supplied global phase becomes a conditional phase and is retained.

For an eigenstate, the independent reference distribution is

P(y)=1N2∣∑k=0N−1e2πik(ϕ−y/N)∣2.P(y)=\frac{1}{N^2}\left|\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}e^{2\pi ik(\phi-y/N)}\right|^2.P(y)=N21​∣∣​k=0∑N−1​e2πik(ϕ−y/N)∣∣​2.
(3)

A phase exactly on the grid is recovered with ideal probability one. For other phases, the two adjacent grid points are within circular error 1/N1/N1/N and together have probability at least 8/π28/\pi^28/π2. The median of 15 independent scalar estimates has majority-failure bound below 0.005 at that single-run bound. Scalar medians require ϕ∈[1/N,1−1/N]\phi\in[1/N,1-1/N]ϕ∈[1/N,1−1/N], so the good interval does not cross the phase branch cut. A circular median is not implemented. If the input is a superposition of eigenstates, the output is the corresponding phase mixture; a good phase-resolution bound does not establish overlap with the desired eigenstate.

Important variants¶

  • Approximate QFT omits small controlled rotations and includes a derived spectral-norm omission bound. It changes circuit approximation and must share the application’s coherent error allowance.

  • Iterative feedback QPE reuses one phase ancilla, measures least-significant output bits first and applies actual classical feedback. It has a separate circuit, notebook and bounded ideal instrument check.

  • Alternative phase-inference procedures, noisy experimental robustness and application-specific fast controlled powers remain planned. Their confidence and access costs are not inferred from these demos.

End-to-end resource analysis¶

Let CU(c)(n)C_U^{(c)}(n)CU(c)​(n) denote the compiled cost of one controlled supplied UUU, and Cprep(n)C_{\rm prep}(n)Cprep​(n) its state preparation cost. Before synthesis, conventional QPE requires

G(n,m)=Cprep(n)+(2m−1)CU(c)(n)+O(m2),Q(n,m)=n+m.G(n,m)=C_{\rm prep}(n)+(2^m-1)C_U^{(c)}(n)+O(m^2),\qquad Q(n,m)=n+m.G(n,m)=Cprep​(n)+(2m−1)CU(c)​(n)+O(m2),Q(n,m)=n+m.
(4)

With RRR independent repeated runs, preparation and the entire measured circuit are charged RRR times; there are RmRmRm phase-readout bits. For phase resolution ϵϕ=2−m\epsilon_\phi=2^{-m}ϵϕ​=2−m, the repeated-access term is O(CU(c)(n)/ϵϕ)O(C_U^{(c)}(n)/\epsilon_\phi)O(CU(c)​(n)/ϵϕ​). Classical median decoding and compilation are excluded from physical runtime and are unmeasured. An application must provide its actual controlled evolution and eigenstate overlap rather than reuse this toy cost.

The arbitrary-angle T cost is an empirical mean fit for random angles, not a concrete synthesis sequence, per-angle worst-case bound or runtime-tail certificate. QFT matrix checks are capped at eight wires, static statevector checks at 14 total wires, and feedback instrument checks at eight quantum/classical bits and 4,096 top-level operations. A circuit generated with --no-sim is explicitly unvalidated at that size.

Verification and formalization¶

The dedicated QFT/QPE suite has 104 cases covering exact transform matrices for one through six wires, inverses, all tested truncation cutoffs, grid and off-grid phases, bit order, coherent global phases, phase mixtures, actual feedback/reset behavior, primitive lowering and rejected opaque/dynamic access. The reference Fourier matrix and Dirichlet distribution are independent mathematical constructions. Additional independent review checked conjugated multi-qubit eigenstates and entangled phase mixtures. These numerical checks cover the tested instances; they are not a theorem for every generator input.

No Lean formalization is integrated or claimed. SQIR/VOQC is a relevant external Rocq/Coq project; linking it does not prove this Qiskit generator, its feedback instrument or its physical compilation. Any future formal badge needs a pinned successful proof build and a stated connection to the exact implementation.
