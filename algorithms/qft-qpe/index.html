Fourier transform and phase estimation

Motivation and intuition¶

Many quantum tasks encode the answer in an eigenphase: an energy in Hamiltonian evolution, an order in modular arithmetic, or a probability in amplitude amplification. A phase is not directly visible in a computational-basis measurement. Phase estimation uses controlled evolution and interference to turn it into a classical binary record Cleve et al., 1998.

The quantum Fourier transform (QFT) combines the phases carried by a register. Phase estimation first prepares a superposition of different evolution lengths, then applies an inverse QFT so that matching phases add and mismatching phases cancel. QFT produces a quantum state; it does not return every entry of a classical Fourier transform in one measurement. This chapter supplies reusable components for the survey’s applications Dalzell et al., 2025.

Implemented scope

Exact and truncated QFT, textbook QPE, one-ancilla feedback QPE, fresh-preparation Hadamard phase estimation, circular-grid Bayesian inference and bounded-bias robust inference have explicit circuits and independent small-instance checks. The Bayesian companion retains eight prescribed ideal-model transcripts; its credible grid-cell arcs do not supply a uniform confidence certificate or physical estimate. Robust inference supplies a conservative conditional guarantee under its independent-Bernoulli/additive-bias promise; experimental calibration and physical costing remain planned. Five static-QPE surface-code projections are computed under the audited conditional mean-fit model. A separate matched three/four-bit feedback comparison costs actual synthesized corrections, every branch’s common reserved schedule and the final classical median under a declared surface-code scenario. Eigenstate preparation for an arbitrary application and certified rotation synthesis remain application responsibilities. No integrated Lean proof is supplied.

The Hamiltonian-access companion checks an explicit noncommuting two-spin propagator, reduced retained-target/feedback instruments and fresh interference fringes. Its 15 tiny ideal checks have a separate saved evidence packet; full application confidence and physical costing remain planned.

Fundamental algorithm and task contract¶

For N=2mN=2^mN=2m, our positive-sign QFT convention is

FN∣x⟩=1N∑y=0N−1e2πixy/N∣y⟩.F_N\lvert x\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt N}\sum_{y=0}^{N-1}e^{2\pi ixy/N}\lvert y\rangle.FN​∣x⟩=N​1​y=0∑N−1​e2πixy/N∣y⟩.
(1)

The circuit uses mmm Hadamards, m(m−1)/2m(m-1)/2m(m−1)/2 controlled-phase gates and ⌊m/2⌋\lfloor m/2\rfloor⌊m/2⌋ swaps. Wire q[0] is the least-significant bit. Swaps are included, so the matrix is the expression above rather than a bit-reversed variant. The inverse reverses the gate order and the phase signs.

QPE accepts an explicit nnn-qubit circuit UUU and an explicit preparation circuit for an eigenstate

U∣ψ⟩=e2πiϕ∣ψ⟩,0≤ϕ<1.U\lvert\psi\rangle=e^{2\pi i\phi}\lvert\psi\rangle,\qquad 0\leq\phi<1.U∣ψ⟩=e2πiϕ∣ψ⟩,0≤ϕ<1.
(2)

It prepares mmm phase qubits with Hadamards, applies controlled U2jU^{2^j}U2j from phase wire jjj, applies FN†F_N^\daggerFN†​ and measures those mmm wires. The decoded integer yyy estimates ϕ\phiϕ by y/Ny/Ny/N. Our generator constructs each power from 2j2^j2j complete controlled calls to the supplied UUU; no power or dense matrix is treated as a free oracle. A supplied global phase becomes a conditional phase and is retained.

For an eigenstate, the independent reference distribution is

P(y)=1N2∣∑k=0N−1e2πik(ϕ−y/N)∣2.P(y)=\frac{1}{N^2}\left|\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}e^{2\pi ik(\phi-y/N)}\right|^2.P(y)=N21​∣∣​k=0∑N−1​e2πik(ϕ−y/N)∣∣​2.
(3)

A phase exactly on the grid is recovered with ideal probability one. For other phases, the two adjacent grid points are within circular error 1/N1/N1/N and together have probability at least 8/π28/\pi^28/π2. The median of 15 independent scalar estimates has majority-failure bound below 0.005 at that single-run bound. Scalar medians require ϕ∈[1/N,1−1/N]\phi\in[1/N,1-1/N]ϕ∈[1/N,1−1/N], so the good interval does not cross the phase branch cut. A circular median is not implemented. If the input is a superposition of eigenstates, the output is the corresponding phase mixture; a good phase-resolution bound does not establish overlap with the desired eigenstate.

Important variants¶

Phase estimation is a family of measurement and inference procedures. The right choice depends on the requested output, the prepared state, available controlled evolution, coherent lifetime and classical processing. Our executed scope includes exact/truncated QFT, textbook QPE, retained-target feedback QPE, fresh-preparation Hadamard inference, bounded circular-grid Bayesian inference and bounded-bias robust inference. The remaining additional methods below are literature-backed implementation plans. Their papers and external software do not validate our circuits or physical resources. Each implemented variant retains its own stated input and evidence scope.

Shared circuit and resource contract¶

For an eigenphase U∣ψ⟩=e2πiϕ∣ψ⟩U|\psi\rangle=e^{2\pi i\phi}|\psi\rangleU∣ψ⟩=e2πiϕ∣ψ⟩, a Hadamard experiment with controlled UkU^kUk and a phase offset β\betaβ has

Pr⁡(0∣k,β)=12[1+Re⁡ ⁣(e−iβ⟨ψ∣Uk∣ψ⟩)].\Pr(0\mid k,\beta)=\tfrac12\left[1+\operatorname{Re}\!\left(e^{-i\beta}\langle\psi|U^k|\psi\rangle\right)\right].Pr(0∣k,β)=21​[1+Re(e−iβ⟨ψ∣Uk∣ψ⟩)].
(4)

Offsets 0 and π/2\pi/2π/2 reveal cosine and sine information. For a mixed spectral input the signal is a weighted sum of eigenphases, rather than one unknown scalar phase. Retaining the target through a measurement sequence and freshly preparing it for every experiment have different joint distributions. The retained-target energy confidence protocol cannot be transferred to a fresh-preparation sampler without a new argument.

For experiments at Hamiltonian times tjt_jtj​, with NjN_jNj​ shots, record both

Tmax⁡=max⁡j∣tj∣,Tevol=∑jNj∣tj∣,T_{\max}=\max_j|t_j|,\qquad T_{\mathrm{evol}}=\sum_jN_j|t_j|,Tmax​=jmax​∣tj​∣,Tevol​=j∑​Nj​∣tj​∣,
(5)

and charge every preparation, actual controlled evolution, synthesis allocation, measurement, reset/feedback and classical fit. Tmax⁡T_{\max}Tmax​ measures the longest evolution demand; TevolT_{\mathrm{evol}}Tevol​ measures accumulated evolution work. Neither equals physical wall time without compiling the gates and accounting for code cycles, factories, repeated initialization and scheduling. A lower coherent depth can require more shots. External implementations to evaluate¶

Qualtran’s pinned textbook QPE supplies decomposition and compositional counts under Apache-2.0. Its Kaiser-window class supplies coefficients, tensor semantics and a resource call graph; that class does not itself provide a preparation-circuit decomposition. A window vector is insufficient evidence of its compiled cost.

Phayes (Apache-2.0) and Sandia pyRPE (BSD-3-Clause/Apache-2.0 options) are classical inference candidates. The quantum experiment and reproducible environment remain our responsibilities. The QCELS/QMEGS comparison repository is a reference: the inspected code draws ideal samples from supplied eigenvalues/populations, and no license was found. We have not adopted its code or dependencies.

The compressed-sensing experiments use ideal spectral samples and classical optimization. No license or environment lock was found in the inspected tracked source, so they remain a reference. The paper’s mathematical assumptions and its off-grid numerical results must be assessed separately Yi et al., 2024.

EarlyFT_QPE is an Apache-2.0 candidate for the circuit-division comparison. Its inspected eftqpe 0.1.0 source includes likelihood/resource models, controlled Qualtran Hubbard and chemistry walk access, and surface-code factory searches. It pins several direct dependencies, including Qualtran 0.4.1, but has no complete transitive environment lock and does not export a complete MSQPE circuit. We have not installed or reproduced it. A future adoption must supply the actual clock/preparation circuit, complete repetitions and readout, an independent small answer, and a new matched resource record Dutkiewicz et al., 2025.

Let CU(c)(n)C_U^{(c)}(n)CU(c)​(n) denote the compiled cost of one controlled supplied UUU, and Cprep(n)C_{\rm prep}(n)Cprep​(n) its state preparation cost. Before synthesis, conventional QPE requires

An application must provide its actual controlled evolution and eigenstate overlap rather than reuse this toy cost.

Verification and formalization¶

The dedicated QFT/QPE suite has 104 cases covering exact transform matrices for one through six wires, inverses, all tested truncation cutoffs, grid and off-grid phases, bit order, coherent global phases, phase mixtures, actual feedback/reset behavior, primitive lowering and rejected opaque/dynamic access. The reference Fourier matrix and Dirichlet distribution are independent mathematical constructions. Additional independent review checked conjugated multi-qubit eigenstates and entangled phase mixtures. These numerical checks cover the tested instances; they are not a theorem for every generator input.

No Lean formalization is integrated or claimed. SQIR/VOQC is a relevant external Rocq/Coq project; linking it does not prove this Qiskit generator, its feedback instrument or its physical compilation. Any future formal badge needs a pinned successful proof build and a stated connection to the exact implementation.

The inspected Lean-QuantumAlg QPE endpoint supplies an additional external reference. Its QuantumPhaseEstimation.main contract covers controlled-unitary access, actual phase-register preparation/readout, circular precision and restoration of a borrowed eigenstate. Controlled UUU and the eigenstate are supplied: the budget charges 2t−12^t-12t−1 oracle calls, rather than compiling their implementation or eigenstate preparation. Its QFT endpoint uses an independent Fourier operator and native gate budgets. This Apache-2.0 snapshot requires Lean 4.33.1 and pins mathlib 4.33.1 in its Lake manifest Zhang et al., 2026. These are inspected source contracts; no kernel build, Python-to-Lean correspondence, synthesized-rotation certificate or physical compilation proof has run here. The framework paper is a preprint, and its source snapshot is distinct from the earlier Shor manuscript discussed in the factoring chapter.
