Robust phase estimation with bounded probability bias

Motivation and intuition¶

Repeated interference experiments can identify an eigenphase even when the measurement probabilities have a bounded systematic distortion. A long controlled evolution magnifies a small phase difference; sine and cosine observations identify its circular angle, and shorter evolutions identify the correct alias. This is the robust phase-estimation idea used in gate calibration Kimmel et al., 2015.

Implemented scope

This companion supplies original explicit fresh-preparation circuits, rational circular inference, a seeded small readout-confusion example and logical scaling. It uses an independently derived conservative finite-sample guarantee. The 2015 paper’s original error analysis is not adopted. Circuits and readout laws are checked numerically; the arithmetic confidence argument is mathematical and is not a reproduced formal proof. Physical estimation and experimental calibration remain planned.

A separate source/export companion has an executed nine-wire primitive export and retained failed/succeeded generation records. That larger circuit was generated, not quantum simulated. Its source counts supply no phase result, synthesis certificate or physical estimate.

Fundamental algorithm and input contract¶

The input is an exactly prepared single eigenstate

U∣ψ⟩=e2πiϕ∣ψ⟩,ϕ∈[0,1).U|\psi\rangle=e^{2\pi i\phi}|\psi\rangle,\qquad\phi\in[0,1).U∣ψ⟩=e2πiϕ∣ψ⟩,ϕ∈[0,1).
(1)

At each power kj=2jk_j=2^jkj​=2j, j=0,…,L−1j=0,\ldots,L-1j=0,…,L−1, use mmm fresh independent trials in each of two quadratures. The ideal zero probabilities are

pc,j=12[1+cos⁡(2πkjϕ)],ps,j=12[1+sin⁡(2πkjϕ)].p_{c,j}=\tfrac12[1+\cos(2\pi k_j\phi)],\qquad p_{s,j}=\tfrac12[1+\sin(2\pi k_j\phi)].pc,j​=21​[1+cos(2πkj​ϕ)],ps,j​=21​[1+sin(2πkj​ϕ)].
(2)

Our probability model specifies an actual Bernoulli probability qb,jq_{b,j}qb,j​ for each batch, with

∣qb,j−pb,j∣≤β<18.|q_{b,j}-p_{b,j}|\le\beta<\tfrac18.∣qb,j​−pb,j​∣≤β<81​.
(3)

Trials are independent and identically distributed within each batch. A single bias bound must hold at every chosen power and quadrature; it may have different signs in different batches. The bound is supplied as an input promise. No experiment estimates it here. State-preparation, controlled-evolution or synthesis errors can enter this contract only after proving that their combined effect satisfies the probability bound. Correlated drift, arbitrary mixed spectral inputs and unbounded power-dependent dephasing are outside this single-phase guarantee.

With zero counts zc,j,zs,jz_{c,j},z_{s,j}zc,j​,zs,j​, construct the exact rational signals

(4)

It begins with ϕ^0=θ~0\widehat\phi_0=\widetilde\theta_0ϕ​0​=θ0​. At power kj>1k_j>1kj​>1, choose a lift (θ~j+ℓ)/kj(\widetilde\theta_j+\ell)/k_j(θj​+ℓ)/kj​ nearest the preceding circular estimate. Every interval decision uses fractions. In particular, accept an integer ℓ\ellℓ only if

ℓ−12<kjϕ^j−1−bjandkjϕ^j−1−aj<ℓ+12.\ell-\tfrac12<k_j\widehat\phi_{j-1}-b_j \quad\text{and}\quad k_j\widehat\phi_{j-1}-a_j<\ell+\tfrac12.ℓ−21​<kj​ϕ​j−1​−bj​andkj​ϕ​j−1​−aj​<ℓ+21​.

This ensures that the same lift is nearest for every angle in the enclosure. Normalize the lifted midpoint modulo one. Zero signal, an enclosure outside its width guard or a half-spacing boundary returns an explicit inconclusive result, retaining all counts and completed decisions. An accepted result does not itself verify the unknown bias promise.

Independently derived conditional confidence¶

Set t=1/8−β>0t=1/8-\beta>0t=1/8−β>0. Hoeffding applied to each of the 2L2L2L empirical proportions gives

Pr⁡(some ∣p^b,j−qb,j∣>t)≤4Lexp⁡(−2mt2).\Pr(\text{some }|\widehat p_{b,j}-q_{b,j}|>t) \le4L\exp(-2mt^2).Pr(some ∣p​b,j​−qb,j​∣>t)≤4Lexp(−2mt2).
(6)

On the complementary event, each empirical signal component differs from its ideal value by at most 2(β+t)=1/42(\beta+t)=1/42(β+t)=1/4. The complex-signal error is at most 2/4\sqrt2/42​/4. Tangent geometry around the unit circle gives an angular error smaller than 1/161/161/16 turn, since

2/4<sin⁡(π/8).\sqrt2/4<\sin(\pi/8).2​/4<sin(π/8).
(7)

The code requires an angle-enclosure width at most 1/961/961/96 turn. Its midpoint therefore adds at most 1/1921/1921/192 turn, giving a fringe-center error below 13/192<r=1/1213/192<r=1/1213/192<r=1/12. The correct next lift is at distance at most 3r/kj3r/k_j3r/kj​ from the preceding estimate: the preceding error contributes 2r/kj2r/k_j2r/kj​, and the new center contributes r/kjr/k_jr/kj​. Because 3r=1/4<1/23r=1/4<1/23r=1/4<1/2, the correct lift is unique. The enclosure width also leaves a strict margin for the interval decision above. Induction yields

dT(ϕ^,ϕ)≤ϵϕ=112 2L−1d_{\mathbb T}(\widehat\phi,\phi)\le\epsilon_\phi =\frac1{12\,2^{L-1}}dT​(ϕ​,ϕ)≤ϵϕ​=122L−11​
(8)

except on an event of probability at most 4Lexp⁡(−2mt2)4L\exp(-2mt^2)4Lexp(−2mt2). Inconclusive outputs belong to that failure event; they are never omitted from a reported study. The returned closed circular arc splits into two rational intervals when it crosses zero. This is a conditional uniform coverage argument over the stated probability model, not a Bayesian credible interval or an empirical coverage estimate.

The planner uses the positive 128-term rational Taylor sum E128(2mt2)≤exp⁡(2mt2)E_{128}(2mt^2)\le\exp(2mt^2)E128​(2mt2)≤exp(2mt2) and accepts a shot count only when

4LE128(2mt2)≤δ.\frac{4L}{E_{128}(2mt^2)}\le\delta.E128​(2mt2)4L​≤δ.
(9)

All ceiling comparisons are exact. The finite-degree bound can be conservative and the finite trial cap can reject a request; the planner does not claim the globally optimal shot count. The angle calculation uses a 32-term alternating arctangent series with its signed next-term remainder, an exact range reduction to magnitude at most 1/21/21/2, and Machin’s identity π=16arctan⁡(1/5)−4arctan⁡(1/239)\pi=16\arctan(1/5)-4\arctan(1/239)π=16arctan(1/5)−4arctan(1/239). Axes and quadrant decisions are exact. No floating-point atan2 determines an alias.

Important variants and the correction¶

The author-corrected paper, dated 21 October 2021, explains that the original π/(2kj)\pi/(2k_j)π/(2kj​) local-error event was insufficient: an alias error could occur even inside that interval. The replacement analysis uses the stricter π/(3kj)\pi/(3k_j)π/(3kj​) condition and supersedes the original Sections V A and V B Kimmel et al., 2021. Our conservative argument above independently controls both neighboring levels. It does not reuse the incorrect constants or claim the paper’s full calibration procedure.

Published robust calibration protocols use other measurement realizations and level-dependent schedules, and discuss root-mean-square error and optimized accumulated evolution work. This implementation uses uniform shots with a union confidence bound. It has an inverse-precision coherent demand plus logarithmic confidence overhead; it does not inherit an optimal total-work Heisenberg-scaling claim. The ideal Hadamard companion has a different ideal-only guarantee, while Bayesian inference reports posterior grid-cell arcs. Neither posterior credibility nor a numerical consistency test establishes this bias-model promise.

Small circuit and executed readout example¶

The circuit family is the explicit nnn-wire product

Un=⨂i=1nP(θ),∣ψ⟩=∣1⟩⊗n,θ=(2πϕnominal)/n.U_n=\bigotimes_{i=1}^{n}P(\theta),\qquad |\psi\rangle=|1\rangle^{\otimes n},\qquad \theta=(2\pi\phi_{\rm nominal})/n.Un​=i=1⨂n​P(θ),∣ψ⟩=∣1⟩⊗n,θ=(2πϕnominal​)/n.
(10)

The angle is stored as binary64. The nominal phase and a numerical diagnostic from the actual stored angles are recorded separately. The constructor rejects unsupported subnormal/underflow cases. This commuting model is classically easy; its known phase supplies an independent reference and never enters count recovery.

Each trial resets every wire, prepares the data with nnn X gates, applies H to the control, repeats the actual controlled base circuit kkk times, optionally applies S†S^\daggerS† for the sine basis, and applies H/readout. Every controlled P(θ)P(\theta)P(θ) is decomposed into three P gates and two CX gates. There is no dense-unitary gate, phase-only symbolic oracle or analytical P(kθ)P(k\theta)P(kθ) shortcut. The code reuses the unchanged original Hadamard circuit implementation.

The maximum coherent base-call demand is kmax⁡=2L−1k_{\max}=2^{L-1}kmax​=2L−1, and the width is n+1n+1n+1. Fault-tolerant costing remains planned¶

This is a bounded probability/readout model, not physical fault tolerance. Continuous rotations are lowered to RZ but unsynthesized. No factory, code-distance, physical-qubit, routing or physical-runtime estimate is supplied, and the frozen surface-code baseline is untouched. Initial reset, X preparation, every controlled gate and final readout appear in the logical circuits and cannot be discarded to fit a static estimator.

A physical adapter must compile the rotations, retain all resets/repetitions/readouts and account for data exposure, factories and scheduling. It must separately bound any resulting preparation, evolution, synthesis and readout probability distortions; their combined allowance reduces the remaining sampling margin ttt. A gate-failure rate alone is not a per-fringe additive-bias certificate. Physical gains against textbook or retained-target QPE remain uncomputed. Neither QQQ nor kmax⁡k_{\max}kmax​ equals physical wall time.

Classical verification and formal scope¶

The small circuit family has an independent analytic answer and full bounded matrix checks. Tests cover off-grid phases, wraparound, axes and octants, adversarial fringe-center offsets within the sufficient condition, zero signals, exact half-spacing ties, malformed/unbounded count iterables, bias/circuit/sample caps and source ledgers checked against actual programs. An independent high-precision Decimal construction checks rational angle enclosures and conservative exponential ceilings. These numerical tests cover their instances; the elementary proof above supplies the general conditional mathematical argument.

No Lean/Rocq proof build or Python-to-formal correspondence is reproduced. The primary paper, exact rational computation, SDK realization and physical compilation are separate evidence layers. Sandia’s pyRPE at e09be5a83762c94de7880daf45230996b9f6cab3 remains an inspected BSD-3-Clause/Apache-2.0 inference reference; it is not installed or imported by this implementation. No external inference code, survey prose or figure is copied.
