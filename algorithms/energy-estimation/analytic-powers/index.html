Analytic powers of dimer evolution

Motivation and fundamental variant¶

The baseline energy protocol implements U2jU^{2^j}U2j by repeated U calls. Our specified dimer Hamiltonian also has an exact gate formula at any allowed time. Replacing the evolution time ttt with 2jt2^j t2jt therefore constructs the power directly, including the controlled identity/shift phase. The code never inserts a matrix-power gate.

build_energy_unitary(...,power=2**j) builds the actual Pauli/parity rotations. build_retained_target_qpe(...,analytic_powers=True) uses one such controlled power circuit per clock bit. This fast-forwarding is specific to the analytically accessible independent-dimer family. It does not give a constant-cost power for an arbitrary molecular Hamiltonian.

End-to-end resource analysis¶

The task, HF preparation, encoding, mmm, RRR, KKK, readout and error allocations stay fixed. Repeated access has KR(2m−1)KR(2^m-1)KR(2m−1) actual base calls; direct powers have KRmKRmKRm actual power circuits. The sum of simulated base periods remains KR(2m−1)KR(2^m-1)KR(2m−1) and must not be confused with gate calls or hardware elapsed time.

With the defaults m=5m=5m=5, R=19R=19R=19 and K=2K=2K=2, these counts are 1,178 versus 190. Fresh-clock width stays 97, with 190 readouts and two HF preparations. Ideal energy resolution and the retained-target median theorem are unchanged. Long evolution angles still require synthesis precision; they are not free physical operations.

The matched comparison preserves both full workloads and their conditional surface-code frontiers. Both use p=10−4p=10^{-4}p=10−4, 100 ns gates, 500 ns measurements, a 0.005 whole-workload physical allowance and 10-5 per-group synthesis-model allowance. The comparison retains the different mean-fit costs and Clifford counts of the actual circuits. Neither report supplies an angle/output certificate, initialization cost or measured hardware runtime.

The explicit generator supports circuit-only higher resolution when its width/operation caps allow it:

python scripts/generate_energy_estimation.py --analytic-powers --phase-bits 12 \
  --no-sim --no-scaling --no-estimate --output-dir artifacts/h2-high-resolution

The ideal grid bound at m=12m=12m=12 is about 0.000965 Ha; this command exports a circuit and marks its simulation and physical projection unperformed. It does not establish chemical accuracy on hardware.

Verification and formalization¶

Actual analytic powers and their controls are compared with independent matrix powers, including scalar phases. Complete small QPE statevectors agree with repeated-access circuits at the same preparation and decoding convention. The numerical checks are scoped to tested powers and inputs; no all-angle synthesis or Lean certificate is provided.
