Structured degree-one DQI

Motivation and status¶

This family makes the decoder and preparation in decoded quantum interferometry concrete as the number of assignment bits grows. The DQI formulation connects a coherently decoded low-weight clause state to an objective-shaped Fourier distribution Jordan et al., 2025. Here the input family has a simple decoder whose actual gates can be generated without enumerating all syndromes.

Implementation status: executed ideal and compiled checks, companion and conditional fixed physical model

On 13 November 2026, one bounded Linux CPU pass completed 39 focused contract tests, new coherent checks at n=2,3n=2,3n=2,3, generation-only export at n=8n=8n=8, and four ordinary notebook cells with locked Python 3.12.14. The pipeline recorded 6.438 seconds before its final seal. This is CPU validation time, not quantum hardware runtime. The wider n≤64n\leq64n≤64 generator remains source-supported; this pass executed its CLI only at n=8n=8n=8.

The separately validated nine-wire default retains its arbitrary-dirty-scratch decoder law, 377 unitary gates and 396 materialized operations, original failed attempt and successful ordinary notebook evidence. It was not rerun. The new clean decoder has a different workspace contract and its own checks; sharing the n=3n=3n=3 ideal output law does not transfer evidence between them. Those historical results remain separate from the 14 November 2026 compiled-circuit increment below. That separate CPU increment passed its compiled-state and ledger checks and an independent saved-output audit. Hardware execution, a complete end-to-end hardware estimate and a formal theorem remain unavailable.

Explicit preparation and reversible decoding¶

The clause state assigns amplitude 1/n+21/\sqrt{n+2}1/n+2​ to the vacuum and each of the n+1n+1n+1 singletons. For target i=0,…,ni=0,\ldots,ni=0,…,n, its source angle is

θi=2arcsin⁡ ⁣(1n+2−i).\theta_i=2\arcsin\!\left(\frac1{\sqrt{n+2-i}}\right).θi​=2arcsin(n+2−i​1​).
(4)

The first target uses RY directly. Each later target rotates only when all preceding clause bits are zero. X conjugations turn those negative controls into positive controls; a conjunction chain computes their AND into clean scratch, controls the rotation, and reverses the chain. Target i≥2i\geq2i≥2 uses 2(i−1)2(i-1)2(i−1) CCX blocks and 2i2i2i X gates. Target one needs no conjunction scratch.

Each controlled RY is emitted as two RY gates and two CX gates. The source rejects a non-finite angle or a binary angle for which 2(θ/2)≠θ2(\theta/2)\ne\theta2(θ/2)=θ before emitting that controlled rotation. This finite representation check does not certify an irrational ideal angle or synthesize it into a fault-tolerant gate set.

Before target iii, the remaining zero-prefix probability is (n+2−i)/(n+2)(n+2-i)/(n+2)(n+2−i)/(n+2). Its rotation creates singleton probability 1/(n+2)1/(n+2)1/(n+2); after the final target the remaining vacuum has the same probability. The ideal signs are positive and the conjunction scratch is uncomputed.

Z on clause nnn supplies the negative sign. The syndrome contains 2n2n2n CX gates: one from clause jjj and one from clause nnn into assignment wire n+1+jn+1+jn+1+j, for each jjj.

Decoding tests each of the nnn unit patterns and the all-ones pattern. For a unit pattern, X conjugations negate its other n−1n-1n−1 syndrome bits. A positive nnn-control toggle computes an AND chain into n−2n-2n−2 clean scratch bits, toggles its clause target, and reverses the chain. It uses 2n−32n-32n−3 CCX blocks, including the single-CCX case n=2n=2n=2. Each CCX is expanded into the exact 15-primitive H/CX/T/T-dagger sequence, with counts 2/6/4/32/6/4/32/6/4/3; no opaque CCX or decoder instruction remains.

For arbitrary clause target ttt, the clean-workspace contract is
