Adiabatic intuition and a digitized anneal

Adiabatic optimization starts from an easily prepared low-energy state and changes the Hamiltonian toward one whose low-energy states encode solutions Farhi et al., 2000. Interference arises through that evolution rather than a classical search over independent layer angles. How slowly the path must be followed depends on its spectral structure and smoothness. There is no generic polynomial runtime promise for an arbitrary optimization problem.

This example uses the same four-vertex cycle and classical MaxCut reference as QAOA. It implements a digitized time-dependent anneal with explicit gates, not an analog-hardware experiment. The target, time, and number of slices are chosen before examining the studies.

The initial state ∣+⟩⊗4|+\rangle^{\otimes4}∣+⟩⊗4 is the ground state of HDH_DHD​ and needs four H gates from ∣0000⟩|0000\rangle∣0000⟩. Minimizing HPH_PHP​ maximizes the cut. Its ground space consists of the two alternating strings 0101 and 1010, checked by enumeration of all 16 assignments. Success is the total measurement probability of that ground space, not a preference for either string.

The fixed main input is T=16,L=256T=16,L=256T=16,L=256 with a predeclared ideal maximum-cut probability target of at least 0.95. Its executed circuit reaches approximately 0.987022729. That is a checked result on this graph and path, rather than an adiabatic-runtime guarantee.

The continuous reference numerically integrates the Schrödinger equation with SciPy 1.18.1 DOP853, using the tolerances and solver diagnostics recorded in the run JSON SciPy Developers, n.d.. DOP853 provides an independent high-accuracy numerical reference; it is not an exact solution or a global error certificate. A separate dense-matrix implementation of the same Strang slices checks the gate construction up to global phase.

Endpoint degeneracy and a symmetry-sector diagnostic¶

The full spectral gap E1(s)−E0(s)E_1(s)-E_0(s)E1​(s)−E0​(s) is zero at s=1s=1s=1, because the two optimal cuts are degenerate. The global bit flip G=X⊗4G=X^{\otimes4}G=X⊗4 commutes with both Hamiltonians, and the initial uniform state lies in its G=+1G=+1G=+1 sector. Exact evolution and the stated digitized gates preserve that symmetry.

The report scans 201 interpolation points and displays both the full gap and the gap within the eight-dimensional G=+1G=+1G=+1 sector. This helps explain why a vanishing full endpoint gap need not describe the accessible-sector behavior of this example. A finite grid does not certify a lower gap bound between its points, an adiabatic runtime theorem, robustness to symmetry-breaking errors, or scaling on another graph.

Readout and complete fixed-circuit workload¶

Fresh ideal readout uses 4,096 samples for each seed 7, 19, and 41, totaling 12,288 modeled preparations of the complete fixed circuit. Each outcome is evaluated against the explicit classical cut predicate. The source probabilities are computed classically; repeated QPU executions are not performed.

Source:
Digitized annealing on an inspectable MaxCut instance

The resource record counts every preparation, driver rotation, edge interaction, and measurement at those repetitions. CPU simulation and continuous-reference work are recorded separately from unmeasured quantum-device latency. The time and slice scans are diagnostic classical computations; they do not silently become hardware executions in the fixed-circuit report.

Statevector simulation needs exponentially many amplitudes, and the dense references need exponentially large matrices. The statevector helper has a configured 12-qubit limit with a hard cap of 16 and T≤32T\le32T≤32; dense and ODE references have a hard eight-qubit cap and T≤32T\le32T≤32. The displayed example uses four qubits. The constructor separately caps graph width at 32, TTT at 64, and LLL at 4,096, subject to an aggregate 100,000-unitary-gate limit. Exporting a larger circuit does not validate its adiabatic quality or imply affordable simulation.

Fault-tolerant projection is discussed in the main chapter. Arbitrary-angle synthesis there is a conditional expected-cost model, not a concrete sequence or a certified cut-probability error bound. A fixed-circuit physical report is also different from the recorded adaptive-trajectory replay.
