Degree-two DQI on graph cycle codes

Motivation and scope¶

Decoded quantum interferometry (DQI) prepares a small collection of clause-error words, encodes their syndromes, and coherently erases the error register. A final Fourier transform combines the signed contributions into an objective-shaped assignment distribution Jordan et al., 2025. Including pairs of clauses gives a quadratic filter: it changes the prepared state, the counter that controls preparation, and the decoder that must erase each pair.

This page implements those degree-two ingredients for binary graph cycle codes. The small example is a frustrated five-cycle, with every edge asking its endpoints to differ. Its optimum is available by a linear classical scan. The example makes coherent preparation, decoding and interference inspectable; it establishes no optimization advantage.

Phase, syndrome and clean decoding¶

Apply Z to each clause with target one. Then apply CX from that clause to each nonroot endpoint’s syndrome wire: A=2m−d0A=2m-d_0A=2m−d0​ CX gates, where d0d_0d0​ is the root degree. C5 uses five Z and eight syndrome CX gates.

Compile the m+(m2)m+\binom m2m+(2m​) singleton/pair candidates once. Each entry stores an nnn-bit syndrome mask and one or two edge indices. For each candidate, X-conjugate the zero syndrome literals, compute an AND chain through clean scratch, toggle the candidate’s one or two clause targets, and reverse the chain and X conjugations. The comparison does not depend on the target contents. Injectivity permits at most one true predicate, and scratch returns to zero after every predicate.

Every CCX expands into the full fifteen-primitive H/CX/T/T-dagger sequence. For example, the actual beginning of C5 preparation below implements the first controlled counter increment. The full QASM and operation stream expose all later preparation, decoding and inverse gates.

Theory boundaries and reusable artifacts¶

C5’s kernel is the length-five repetition code after a syndrome-basis change, and its signs are affine-equivalent to an eligible repetition scaffold. The new mechanism is its degree-two state preparation and clean two-error circuit; it does not create a new hard code family.

Girth five proves injectivity on D2D_2D2​, but supplies neither useful generic output success nor growing-degree decoding. At ℓ=2,d=5\ell=2,d=5ℓ=2,d=5, C5 also lies outside the stronger ℓ≤d/2−1\ell\leq d/2-1ℓ≤d/2−1 regime quoted by Rosmanis version 1. That paper’s near-linear OPI result retains its QRAMC/QRAMQ access assumptions; this compiled ordinary graph circuit implements no such OPI memory model Rosmanis, 2026.

The every-neighbor recovery premise in Apte and colleagues’ version 2 fails for every weight-two C5 word. Add a missing edge to make a weight-three word: its weight-two complement has the same syndrome, since the full five-cycle is a kernel word. This decoder returns the complement rather than that weight-three neighbor. Correct decoding of the prepared shell therefore does not meet that premise, and no semicircle theorem is used here. The paper’s field/channel/ensemble and classical-decoder qualifications remain attached to its results Apte et al., 2026. These two works are cited as exact preprint versions; peer-reviewed successors remain unverified in the project’s reading scope.
