Degree-two DQI on graph cycle codes

Motivation and scope¶

Decoded quantum interferometry (DQI) prepares a small collection of clause-error words, encodes their syndromes, and coherently erases the error register. A final Fourier transform combines the signed contributions into an objective-shaped assignment distribution Jordan et al., 2025. Including pairs of clauses gives a quadratic filter: it changes the prepared state, the counter that controls preparation, and the decoder that must erase each pair.

This page implements those degree-two ingredients for binary graph cycle codes. The small example is a frustrated five-cycle, with every edge asking its endpoints to differ. Its optimum is available by a linear classical scan. The example makes coherent preparation, decoding and interference inspectable; it establishes no optimization advantage.

Accepted finite component and separate saved companion

On 14 November 2026, 170 focused pure tests passed, the five-cycle source circuit was generated, and its independent verifier completed one twelve-wire forward evolution, 512 Boolean clean-target decoder mappings and one small phase-exact CCX reference. A figure-eight circuit was exported separately without simulation. The original combined sequence then failed at its notebook stage, because serialized source arrays reached a client expecting text.

It joined source arrays in memory, preserved the original cell IDs/types and source projection, and displayed existing evidence. It repeated no tests, circuit generation, state evolution, decoder check or phase reference. The original failed sequence remains failed and spent. Acceptance covers the finite C5 component and this separate genuine companion; it is not a reclassification of the original whole sequence.

The accepted component record records these separate events. Larger supported inputs remain generator scope; synthesis, physical resources, hardware execution and local formal proof remain unavailable.

Phase, syndrome and clean decoding¶

Apply Z to each clause with target one. Then apply CX from that clause to each nonroot endpoint’s syndrome wire: A=2m−d0A=2m-d_0A=2m−d0​ CX gates, where d0d_0d0​ is the root degree. C5 uses five Z and eight syndrome CX gates.

Compile the m+(m2)m+\binom m2m+(2m​) singleton/pair candidates once. Each entry stores an nnn-bit syndrome mask and one or two edge indices. For each candidate, X-conjugate the zero syndrome literals, compute an AND chain through clean scratch, toggle the candidate’s one or two clause targets, and reverse the chain and X conjugations. The comparison does not depend on the target contents. Injectivity permits at most one true predicate, and scratch returns to zero after every predicate.
