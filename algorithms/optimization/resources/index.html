The ledger also stores each exact evaluation’s parameters, logical gates and depth. These simulated circuit counts are separate from shot-weighted readout counts. Arbitrary rotations remain unsynthesized; physical qubits, T counts, factories and runtime are pending. Reset timing, routing, graph/input transfer, noise and mitigation are excluded. Reference checks are included in whole CPU workflow time but are not timed separately.

The run record preserves initialization, optimizer sweeps, every accepted/rejected objective call, seeds, fitted parameters and CPU timings. The whole CPU workflow timer includes its toy reference checks, which are not separately timed; export/plot/file I/O is outside that timer.

The logical ledger keeps exact evaluation circuits separate from modeled shot-weighted readout. QAOA measures the joint cut score in the computational basis. VQE shares shots between its Z terms, retains their covariance, and uses an independent X-basis group. Preparation, basis changes, each circuit and individual qubit readouts are included in the declared logical workload.

A physical projection must account for each parameter setting and measurement group, synthesis choices, all repetitions, classical-control latency and achieved output quality. Multiplying a final ansatz by the number of exact optimizer calls does not establish a real finite-shot trajectory. The next worker increment addresses that missing layer.

The shared rotation adapter supplies conditional mean-fit cost projections, not certified synthesis or an optimizer-convergence guarantee. A confidence statement for one fixed bounded scalar mean does not silently cover adaptive parameters or a noncommuting energy sum. Any later projection must preserve those distinctions and the fault-tolerance appendix’s separate budgets.
