qDRIFT: randomized Hamiltonian evolution

Motivation and intuition¶

qDRIFT replaces a prescribed product-formula ordering with randomly chosen term exponentials. Larger coefficients are selected more often. Averaging over newly drawn complete circuits approximates the desired dynamics even when one circuit is a poor approximation to the evolution operator Campbell, 2019.

Evidence and output contract

Executed evidence consists of bounded ideal circuit/channel checks and seeded classical sampling, with actual circuit exports and a reproduced companion notebook. The small study contains one readout from each freshly selected trajectory. Its outputs are a channel and a measured observable, rather than one deterministic coherent evolution oracle. Physical estimates and formalization remain planned.

Fundamental algorithm and complexity¶

For a supplied Pauli expansion,

H=∑j=1mhjPj,λ=∑j∣hj∣,pj=∣hj∣/λ,H=\sum_{j=1}^{m}h_jP_j,\qquad\lambda=\sum_j|h_j|,\qquad p_j=|h_j|/\lambda,H=j=1∑m​hj​Pj​,λ=j∑​∣hj​∣,pj​=∣hj​∣/λ,
(1)

choose jjj independently at every step and apply

Uj=exp⁡[−i sgn⁡(hj)λtPj/N].U_j=\exp[-i\,\operatorname{sgn}(h_j)\lambda tP_j/N].Uj​=exp[−isgn(hj​)λtPj​/N].
(2)

A complete trajectory has NNN such gates in chronological order. For column-vectorized density matrices, the averaged elementary channel and complete channel are

S=∑jpj(Uj‾⊗Uj),EN=SN.S=\sum_jp_j(\overline{U_j}\otimes U_j),\qquad\mathcal E_N=S^N.S=j∑​pj​(Uj​​⊗Uj​),EN​=SN.
(3)

The algorithm’s channel-error complexity scales as O(λ2t2/ϵ)O(\lambda^2t^2/\epsilon)O(λ2t2/ϵ) term exponentials in the stated ideal access model Campbell, 2019. This asymptotic relation is not a certificate that our chosen small NNN meets a specified diamond-error target, or that one saved circuit has operator error ϵ\epsilonϵ.

The code treats decimal/rational strings, including str(float), as exact fractions. It combines repeated Pauli labels before computing λ\lambdaλ and drops exactly zero coefficients. Integer cumulative weights implement the declared categorical probabilities without a floating-point probability table. PRNG seeds support reproduction; mathematical independence remains an explicit ideal-randomness assumption. Identity terms stay in the distribution and contribute circuit global phase.

Primitive rotations and input access¶

For a Pauli with kkk nonidentity wires, X wires use H basis changes, and Y wires use SDG–H followed by H–S on the way back. A parity ladder uses 2(k−1)2(k-1)2(k−1) CX gates around one RZ(2sgn⁡(hj)λt/N)R_Z(2\operatorname{sgn}(h_j)\lambda t/N)RZ​(2sgn(hj​)λt/N). The generator appends these gates directly. It never inserts a dense Hamiltonian or an opaque evolution gate.

The measured trial resets all nnn wires, prepares the declared computational basis state with X gates, runs every exponential, and measures every wire in Z. Coefficients and Pauli strings are explicit classical inputs. Their table construction and all term draws remain classical work; no data-loading oracle is assumed free.

Every selected circuit is compared with an independent dense product using P2=IP^2=IP2=I. The actual reset/preparation prefix is also simulated as a density operator and compared with that product’s prepared state. A separate single-step mixture is raised to NNN without enumerating exponentially many complete schedules. Tests independently enumerate every two-step trajectory for a tiny noncommuting example and check the full channel on matrix units, rather than only the zero-state observable.

The records distinguish the averaged channel, the empirical average of saved circuit channels and individual trajectory means. The plots show channel-error curves, individual trajectory expectations, the ideal mixture mean and the mean of one classically sampled full Z-readout per trajectory, scored by bit zero. These are ideal simulation results, not QPU measurements.

The reported spectral superoperator norm is the Hilbert–Schmidt-induced 2→22\to22→2 norm. Normalized Choi trace distance, fixed-input trace distance and observable bias are separate diagnostics. None is a computed diamond norm. CP/TP follow analytically from a mixture of ideal unitary channels; floating-point Choi eigenvalue and trace checks are implementation diagnostics, not interval or formal certificates.

Important variants and sampling contracts¶

Repeating readout on one saved seed estimates that circuit’s expectation. Freshly drawing a complete schedule before each readout instead estimates the averaged-channel expectation. Those experiments have different joint distributions and must not share an unsupported confidence claim.

For ideal independent ±1\pm1±1 readouts from fresh trajectories, Hoeffding gives

R≥⌈2log⁡(2/η)ϵstat2⌉.R\ge\left\lceil\frac{2\log(2/\eta)}{\epsilon_{\rm stat}^2}\right\rceil.R≥⌈ϵstat2​2log(2/η)​⌉.
(4)

The 32-trial examples deliberately have broad confidence intervals. Their seeded realization does not empirically validate the analytic coverage statement. Hamiltonian channel bias, scalar gate rounding, synthesis error, statistical precision and physical failures have separate budgets. Controlled qDRIFT for phase estimation, grouped/importance-sampled variants and hardware-specific compilation remain planned.

End-to-end resource analysis¶

Space and time as the system grows¶

The logical width is nnn data wires with no ancillas for these Pauli circuits. One support-kjk_jkj​ sampled exponential uses one RZ, 2(kj−1)2(k_j-1)2(kj​−1) CX and its actual basis changes; an identity term carries only global phase. The saved ledger sums the actual instructions of every complete trajectory rather than multiplying one favorable circuit by the trial count.

For RRR fresh trials, charge RRR complete preparations, RNRNRN categorical draws/exponentials, every basis/parity gate and RnRnRn final measurements. Explicit input processing depends on mmm, Pauli representation and the bit length of the integer weight table. The generator limits that representation independently of dense simulation.

For the local chain with X and optional Z fields,

λ=∣J∣(n−1)+n∣hX∣+n∣hZ∣.\lambda=|J|(n-1)+n|h_X|+n|h_Z|.λ=∣J∣(n−1)+n∣hX​∣+n∣hZ​∣.
(5)

Each local term has bounded support, so NNN exponentials require O(N+n)O(N+n)O(N+n) gates per measured trial before synthesis. At fixed couplings and time, the ideal qDRIFT query scaling can consequently grow quadratically with nnn for a fixed channel-error target. Arbitrary dense Pauli strings can add O(n)O(n)O(n) work to each exponential. These source scalings do not establish an advantage over another compiled algorithm.

Fault-tolerant handoff¶

No new physical estimate was executed. The next surface-code adapter must cost the complete measured workload, including resets, all draws’ circuit constructions, routing/idle exposure, readout and factories. Reset and feedforward assumptions must be explicit; an adapter that drops the initial resets does not cost the exported trial.

The scalar RZ conversion bound recorded in the ledger concerns binary64 angle conversion only. It excludes a concrete Clifford+T synthesis proof and Qiskit’s overall-phase accumulation/reduction for identity terms. Overall phase is immaterial to these measured channels, but exported QASM must not silently become a coherently controlled oracle. Any later physical comparison will preserve the frozen task, output, accuracy, success and hardware assumptions.

Classical simulation and formalization¶

The circuit generator stops at 64 wires, 256 input terms, 512 exponentials and 100,000 primitive operations. Dense trajectory/channel references stop at three qubits before allocation; an empirical study stops at 64 trajectories. A density matrix or unitary has 4n4^n4n entries, while the full superoperator has 16n16^n16n entries. These limits are deliberately different from circuit materialization limits.

No Lean or Rocq proof is reproduced for qDRIFT here. The algebraic channel identity and numerical checks do not establish a compiler or physical fault-tolerance theorem.

Reproduce, packages and downloads¶

Use Python 3.12 and the locked environment. Preserve the recorded output and write each new run to a fresh directory:

python scripts/generate_simulation_qdrift.py --output-dir artifacts/qdrift-replay
pytest tests/test_simulation_qdrift.py
python scripts/generate_simulation_qdrift.py --qubits 32 --steps 64 \
  --no-sim --output-dir artifacts/qdrift-chain-32
