Generalized QSP with supplied angles

Status: checked bounded component

The supplied-angle construction has 37 completed dedicated tests, six small circuit/reference cases, two generation-only exports and five genuinely executed notebook cells. Explicit signal access, complete reset/preparation/ readout and the complex polynomial convention are included. An inverse angle compiler, complete Hamiltonian-simulation task, certified rotation synthesis, physical-resource estimate and formal proof remain planned.

Motivation and intuition¶

Generalized quantum signal processing (GQSP) interleaves general single-qubit rotations with a unitary signal controlled by one ancilla. Interference creates a complex polynomial of the signal, including both even and odd powers. Motlagh and Wiebe establish the polynomial-pair characterization for this mechanism Motlagh & Wiebe, 2024. Our first circuit supplies the angles and constructs the resulting polynomial; it does not solve the inverse problem of finding angles for a requested function.

Fundamental algorithm and declared convention¶

The explicit signal is

Wn=eiχ(∏j=0n−2CZj,j+1)(⨂j=0n−1RZ(2zj)RY(2yj)).W_n=e^{i\chi}\left(\prod_{j=0}^{n-2}\mathrm{CZ}_{j,j+1}\right) \left(\bigotimes_{j=0}^{n-1}R_Z(2z_j)R_Y(2y_j)\right).Wn​=eiχ(j=0∏n−2​CZj,j+1​)(j=0⨂n−1​RZ​(2zj​)RY​(2yj​)).
(1)

Every data wire has actual rotations. The entangling layer has actual gates; no dense matrix or symbolic oracle is appended. This family is a unitary signal example, with its own size parameter. It is not an implementation of the evolution of an arbitrary specified Hamiltonian.

Ancilla q0 is the least-significant wire, and data wire j occupies q(j+1). The signal operation is

D(W)=∣0⟩⟨0∣⊗W+∣1⟩⟨1∣⊗I.D(W)=|0\rangle\langle0|\otimes W+|1\rangle\langle1|\otimes I.D(W)=∣0⟩⟨0∣⊗W+∣1⟩⟨1∣⊗I.
(2)

The implementation uses the determinant-one Euler convention

Ej=RZ(ϕj)RY(2θj)RZ(λj).E_j=R_Z(\phi_j)R_Y(2\theta_j)R_Z(\lambda_j).Ej​=RZ​(ϕj​)RY​(2θj​)RZ​(λj​).
(3)

P0=a0,Q0=c0,Pj(z)=ajzPj−1(z)+bjQj−1(z),Qj(z)=cjzPj−1(z)+djQj−1(z).P_0=a_0,\quad Q_0=c_0,\qquad P_j(z)=a_jzP_{j-1}(z)+b_jQ_{j-1}(z),\quad Q_j(z)=c_jzP_{j-1}(z)+d_jQ_{j-1}(z).P0​=a0​,Q0​=c0​,Pj​(z)=aj​zPj−1​(z)+bj​Qj−1​(z),Qj​(z)=cj​zPj−1​(z)+dj​Qj−1​(z).
(4)

The source therefore constructs first-column blocks P(W),Q(W)P(W),Q(W)P(W),Q(W) of degree at most d. Unitarity gives the ideal real-parameter identity ∣P(z)∣2+∣Q(z)∣2=1|P(z)|^2+|Q(z)|^2=1∣P(z)∣2+∣Q(z)∣2=1 on ∣z∣=1|z|=1∣z∣=1. The numerical checks evaluate selected points and do not constitute a uniform floating-point certificate.

The paper’s arXiv v2 Equation 6 uses a different phase/sign gauge. If that matrix is denoted RpR_pRp​, direct entry comparison gives

E(θ,ϕ,λ)=ei((ϕ+λ)/2+π)Rp(θ,−ϕ,−λ−π).E(\theta,\phi,\lambda) =e^{i((\phi+\lambda)/2+\pi)} R_p(\theta,-\phi,-\lambda-\pi).E(θ,ϕ,λ)=ei((ϕ+λ)/2+π)Rp​(θ,−ϕ,−λ−π).

This explicit factor matters to complex amplitudes. The implementation retains its own Euler convention and forward recurrence throughout; it does not silently substitute the paper’s angle arrays.

Small circuit and independent checks¶

Choose W=eiπ/4RZ(π/2)W=e^{i\pi/4}R_Z(\pi/2)W=eiπ/4RZ​(π/2), whose eigenvalues are 1 and i, and

E0=(θ,ϕ,λ)=(π/4,0,0),E1=(π/4,0,π/2).E_0=(\theta,\phi,\lambda)=(\pi/4,0,0),\qquad E_1=(\pi/4,0,\pi/2).E0​=(θ,ϕ,λ)=(π/4,0,0),E1​=(π/4,0,π/2).

P(z)=12(e−iπ/4z−eiπ/4),Q(z)=12(e−iπ/4z+eiπ/4).P(z)=\tfrac12(e^{-i\pi/4}z-e^{i\pi/4}),\qquad Q(z)=\tfrac12(e^{-i\pi/4}z+e^{i\pi/4}).P(z)=21​(e−iπ/4z−eiπ/4),Q(z)=21​(e−iπ/4z+eiπ/4).

End-to-end resources and fault-tolerant analysis¶

A zero-controlled signal is built from two X wrappers, controlled rotations, compiled three-wire CCZ phases and a P(χ\chiχ) on the control inside the wrapper. The signal’s global phase becomes a conditional phase. Ordinary signal exports use a literal phase-identity gate sequence, so the source and exported complete trials have zero omitted overall phase.

In this unoptimized source construction, one controlled signal has 25n−1425n-1425n−14 named primitive instructions: four operations for each controlled Y/Z rotation, 17 for each CCZ, two X wrappers and one conditional P. The d-signal body therefore has

Gbody=d(25n−14)+3(d+1),Q=n+1.G_{\rm body}=d(25n-14)+3(d+1),\qquad Q=n+1.Gbody​=d(25n−14)+3(d+1),Q=n+1.
(8)

Input preparation adds 0, 1, 2 or 3 gates for the supported basis or H/S/X inputs. A complete measured trial also resets and measures all n+1 wires. The saved complete circuits match these source expressions. They do not count physical cycles or synthesized T sequences for the parameterized rotations. The literal CCZ constructions separately use seven T/Tdag instructions per edge and signal call.

Classical simulation, formalization and bounds¶

The source generator caps data wires at 64, degree at 64, angles at magnitude 32π and complete source size at 120,000 instructions. Dense checks are separately limited to four total wires and 5,000 body instructions. The suite’s n=8 and n=64, d=6 exports are generation-only. A generated large circuit would not establish its numerical or physical correctness.

Forward coefficient construction is bounded classical preprocessing. It is neither an inverse phase compiler nor a proof kernel. The optional companion notebook has five executed genuine code cells, including a circuit drawing and independent small comparison. No Lean or Rocq proof is reproduced. Qiskit 2.6.2 supplies gate and simulator infrastructure under Apache-2.0 Qiskit contributors, n.d.; this prototype’s construction and references are original MIT source. The existing lock remains unchanged.

The 37 original test outcomes and public evidence summary retain the validation scope. Tests and the suite passed in an earlier full run that then failed during notebook readiness/process observation. A separate unchanged-source notebook-only continuation completed the five cells. These events remain distinct; the original failed run is not relabeled as a successful full workflow.

Reproduce and download¶

Download the flat original-code package, or keep the original generator, module, environment lock and MIT license beside the original executed notebook. Start Jupyter in that folder. The generator selects its explicit sibling module and needs no private repository or planning files in this layout.

python -m pip install -r requirements-lock.txt
python -B generate_simulation_gqsp.py --suite --output-dir ../gqsp-recomputed
python -B generate_simulation_gqsp.py --n 64 --degree 6 --no-sim \
  --output-dir ../gqsp-n64-recomputed

Choose fresh output directories outside the downloaded folder. Wider requests require --no-sim; supplied angles, construction and simulation caps remain explicit. The saved-file checker reads hashes and stored evidence without rerunning circuits. This public folder’s detached-mode portability check is separate from the completed scientific checks and has not yet executed.

The archived notebook retains its original pre-execution “planned/prototype” introduction to preserve exact source and output bytes. Its stored execution counts 1–5 and original outputs are the actual completed evidence. The frozen source notebook, original suite manifest and public manifest make the source/data relationship inspectable.
