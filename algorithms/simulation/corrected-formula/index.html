Corrected product formula with explicit pair boundaries

Status: small circuit and classical checks executed

A separately completed genuine notebook executed its five original code cells, including one- and two-pair checks and the circuit drawing. Larger circuits below are generation-only. Motivation and intuition¶

A product formula’s short alternating evolutions introduce commutator errors. A corrector rotates the computational frame around that formula to cancel a selected leading error. A symplectic corrector has an inverse on its other side, so equal intermediate boundary operations cancel over a fixed sequence. The remaining first and last operations still cost gates.

This page selects the first symplectic correction to Strang splitting from Bagherimehrab and colleagues Bagherimehrab et al., 2026. The model has disjoint two-spin pairs: it is classically factorized and provides a transparent circuit check, with no application-advantage claim.

Classical checks and formalization¶

The 24 executed tests compare all complex entries, without phase alignment, against independently written 4×44\times44×4 Pauli references. For each pair, Hj2=(aj2+α2bj2)IH_j^2=(a_j^2+\alpha^2b_j^2)IHj2​=(aj2​+α2bj2​)I gives a closed-form exact evolution. A separate full bit-basis Hamiltonian and SciPy exponential check that reference, while a closed-form Strang matrix checks chronology. The producer requires the independent-reference, preparation and reset discrepancies to be finite and at most 10-11.

Other executed checks cover the seven-gate YZ identity, corrector conjugation, a signed two-pair model, a complex correlated input, preparation and reset from a mixed input, and actual classical readout mappings. Missing and wrong-sign boundary controls are frozen on a noncommuting one-pair case; their saved full-complex-operator contrasts are 0.0275952 for missing boundaries and 0.0549697 for wrong-sign boundaries; the correct construction’s entry discrepancy is 3.51083×10−163.51083\times10^{-16}3.51083×10−16. These are fixed numerical controls, not formal or hardware certificates. The complete control record retains its exact model and values.

No Lean/Rocq proof is supplied. The paper’s mathematical result and the independent small numerical checks do not prove full implementation correctness for all supported inputs.
