Thermal and Gibbs state preparation

Implementation status

An explicit diagonal two-qubit Gibbs purification and its reduced thermal state are checked against an independent normalized matrix exponential. Separate circuits retain the mixed system state or produce measured thermal trials. Classical coefficient loading is exhaustive and grows exponentially. A separate noncommuting thermal-filter example applies actual signed Pauli access to a Bell purification. General scalable filtering/mixing, certified synthesis and physical estimates remain planned.

Motivation and intuition¶

A thermal mixed state favors low-energy configurations without selecting one ground state. A purification stores those probabilities as amplitudes entangled with a reference; tracing out the reference gives the mixed state. Noncommuting Hamiltonians require an actual quantum filter or other preparation procedure rather than direct classical basis weights.

For a stated finite Hamiltonian and inverse temperature β\betaβ in inverse energy units, the thermal target is

ρβ=e−βHZβ,Zβ=Tr⁡(e−βH).\rho_\beta=\frac{e^{-\beta H}}{Z_\beta},\qquad Z_\beta=\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-\beta H}).ρβ​=Zβ​e−βH​,Zβ​=Tr(e−βH).
(1)

This page distinguishes that density operator, its purification, and measured thermal observables. Algorithmic Gibbs preparation requires access, normalization and spectral assumptions; the original preparation methods provide context Poulin & Wocjan, 2009. Our first circuit instead uses an explicitly enumerated diagonal model and pays its classical coefficient-loading cost.

Fundamental algorithm and small circuit¶

The original generator uses a binary tree of conditional probability masses. At each prefix it applies an explicit controlled RYR_YRY​ rotation with angle 2atan2⁡(pright,pleft)2\operatorname{atan2}(\sqrt{p_{\rm right}},\sqrt{p_{\rm left}})2atan2(pright​​,pleft​​), preparing

∑xpx∣x⟩S.\sum_x\sqrt{p_x}|x\rangle_S.x∑​px​​∣x⟩S​.
(4)

System-to-reference CX gates then produce

∣Ψβ⟩=∑xpx∣x⟩S∣x⟩R,Tr⁡R∣Ψβ⟩⟨Ψβ∣=ρβ.|\Psi_\beta\rangle=\sum_x\sqrt{p_x}|x\rangle_S|x\rangle_R,\qquad \operatorname{Tr}_R|\Psi_\beta\rangle\langle\Psi_\beta|=\rho_\beta.∣Ψβ​⟩=x∑​px​​∣x⟩S​∣x⟩R​,TrR​∣Ψβ​⟩⟨Ψβ​∣=ρβ​.
(5)

Controlled-rotation state loading is a standard constructive approach Möttönen et al., 2005; the code here is original. It contains explicit gates rather than an opaque state-preparation or dense-unitary operation. System wires are q0,q1 and reference wires q2,q3.

There are three different output schedules:

OutputCircuit and interpretation
PurificationPrepare the pure system-reference state; leave every wire unmeasured
Retained mixed systemInitialize all wires, purify, then irreversibly reset the reference without conditioning; leave system unmeasured
Measured thermal trialInitialize all wires, purify, measure system wires in Z, and discard the reference without conditioning

The reference must be traced out, discarded or reset without conditioning to obtain the thermal marginal. Uncomputing the copying CX gates restores a coherent amplitude state, not a Gibbs density operator. Conditioning on reference outcomes gives basis states rather than the thermal mixture. The separate reset and readout circuits make this distinction concrete.

End-to-end resource analysis¶

Ideal preparation succeeds deterministically: there is no postselection, amplification or required input-overlap estimate. The cost is moved into exhaustive classical normalization and angle loading. The four-qubit purification lowers to six CX, five arbitrary RY and two X gates, with circuit dependency depth 10. A retained mixed-state preparation adds four initial reset instructions and two reference reset instructions. A measured thermal trial adds four initial resets and two system measurements, and discards two reference wires. The exported circuits include those actual schedules.

Representation and ideal preparation error, coherent synthesis error, statistical confidence and physical faults are separate. The ledger proposes a per-trial coherent operator allowance of 0.005, giving a conservative 0.01 bias allowance for a Z0Z_0Z0​ mean, independently of its 0.03 statistical error. A separate proposed physical-failure probability of 0.005 across all trials is divided by the trial count. These budgets have not been realized by synthesis or a physical model. The angles remain unsynthesized; no concrete Clifford+T sequence or physical-error confidence is claimed. Reset/discard/readout wall time, hardware scheduling, factories, error correction and physical resources are unavailable. Classical energy/Boltzmann preprocessing and circuit materialization wall times are recorded by the generator, but they are not quantum runtime.

Run, inspect and scale¶

python scripts/generate_simulation_gibbs.py
pytest tests/test_simulation_gibbs.py

The parameterized loader accepts explicit diagonal energy lists of at most eight system qubits, and the generated purification doubles that width. Dense circuit/reference checks stop at six system qubits. The floating preparation domain requires β(Emax⁡−Emin⁡)≤700\beta(E_{\max}-E_{\min})\leq700β(Emax​−Emin​)≤700, and exported primitive circuits are capped at 250,000 operations. These are materialization and numerical limits, not a statement of asymptotic feasibility.

The notebook provides the labeled outputs above and optional live temperature controls. Static execution with OPENQUANTUM_STATIC_BUILD=1 creates no widget objects. Independent notebook downloads need gibbs.py beside the notebook and the locked dependencies.
