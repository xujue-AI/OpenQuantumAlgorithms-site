Qubitization and quantum signal processing

Motivation and intuition¶

A product formula approximates evolution by many short term rotations. Quantum signal processing instead transforms a block encoding of the Hamiltonian into a polynomial in its normalized eigenvalues. Ancilla phase rotations choose the polynomial; repeated accesses apply it to every spectral component without loading eigenvectors. Qubitization supplies the invariant two-dimensional spaces on which that signal-processing picture applies Low & Chuang, 2019Low & Chuang, 2017.

This page belongs to Hamiltonian dynamics. It distinguishes the checked cosine-filter primitive, the complex cosine/sine combination, and oblivious amplification. Their normalization and success costs differ.

Qubitization and QSP cosine filtering¶

The five Hamiltonian terms have α=∑j∣cj∣=4.1\alpha=\sum_j|c_j|=4.1α=∑j​∣cj​∣=4.1. Explicit PREPARE and signed Pauli SELECT give the Hermitian involution

B=PREPARE†SELECT PREPARE,⟨0∣B∣0⟩=H/α.B=\mathrm{PREPARE}^\dagger\mathrm{SELECT}\,\mathrm{PREPARE},\qquad\langle0|B|0\rangle=H/\alpha.B=PREPARE†SELECTPREPARE,⟨0∣B∣0⟩=H/α.
(1)

Tests check the actual block. A reflection about the zero-label subspace creates the qubitization walk and its invariant-subspace identity is checked Low & Chuang, 2019.

The QSP module documents its scalar matrix convention and phase ordering. Fourteen calls to BBB and fifteen frozen phases produce a degree-14 real polynomial approximating cos⁡(4.1x)/2\cos(4.1x)/2cos(4.1x)/2 on [−1,1][-1,1][−1,1]. A flag coherently combines the +ϕ+\phi+ϕ and −ϕ-\phi−ϕ branches to extract the real part, sharing block calls. The phase JSON records a deterministic SciPy fit, independent dense-grid residual, Chebyshev-coefficient discrepancy and analytic polynomial-tail bound. Tests compare the actual circuit block to cos⁡(H)/2\cos(H)/2cos(H)/2, separately from the scalar phase check Low & Chuang, 2017Gilyén et al., 2019.

Complex QSP dynamics with explicit postselection¶

The next circuit completes the cosine/sine combination for the fixed dynamics task. Freeze an even degree-14 phase sequence for C(x)≈cos⁡(4.1x)/2C(x)\approx\cos(4.1x)/2C(x)≈cos(4.1x)/2 and an odd degree-15 sequence for S(x)≈sin⁡(4.1x)/2S(x)\approx\sin(4.1x)/2S(x)≈sin(4.1x)/2. A uniform selector and a relative −i-i−i phase on its sine branch give the zero-selector block

K(x)=C(x)−iS(x)2≈e−i4.1x4.K(x)=\frac{C(x)-iS(x)}{2}\approx\frac{e^{-i4.1x}}{4}.K(x)=2C(x)−iS(x)​≈4e−i4.1x​.
(2)

This is the QSP/LCU normalization, not a deterministic unitary on the system Low & Chuang, 2017Gilyén et al., 2019Martyn et al., 2021. The generator shares fourteen ordinary BBB calls between the branches, adds one actual selector-controlled PREPARE/SELECT/inverse-PREPARE call, and implements every selector-conditioned projector phase. It does not append a matrix exponential gate. All three label bits, the real-part flag and the selector must be zero to accept; all relative branch phases are included in the circuit and in decomposition checks.

Complexity and end-to-end resource analysis¶

For an explicit Pauli Hamiltonian H=∑j=0L−1cjPjH=\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}c_jP_jH=∑j=0L−1​cj​Pj​, the input normalization is α=∑j∣cj∣\alpha=\sum_j|c_j|α=∑j​∣cj​∣. Let CB(n,L)C_B(n,L)CB​(n,L) be the elementary cost of this page’s actual PREPARE/SELECT/unprepare access and CΠC_\PiCΠ​ the success-register reflection/phase cost. A degree-ddd sequence uses ddd block accesses and d+1d+1d+1 projector phases. Thus its circuit cost scales as dCB+(d+1)CΠdC_B+(d+1)C_\PidCB​+(d+1)CΠ​, plus input preparation, parity selection and readout. A query count alone omits both access costs.

The implemented label-tree preparation and indexed Pauli SELECT explicitly enumerate the supplied terms. Label width grows as ⌈log⁡2L⌉\lceil\log_2 L\rceil⌈log2​L⌉, while controlled gate decomposition and state-loading work grow with the actual term list. This is a bounded explicit-access generator, not an assumed constant-time Hamiltonian oracle. For a local spin chain L=O(n)L=O(n)L=O(n) and α\alphaα grows with nnn at fixed couplings; fixed time therefore changes αt\alpha tαt and the required polynomial/phase sequence. Reusing degree 14/15 at every size would not preserve accuracy.

The downloaded complex circuit ledger counts the eight-wire demo and capped complete attempts. The amplification ledger counts all forward/inverse accesses, reflections and rejected trials. At rrr integer amplification rounds, the coherent access is used 2r+12r+12r+1 times and the actual success projection is reflected 2r2r2r times. Round count is a separate parameter from Hamiltonian size and degree.

The fixed demo’s numerical approximation and acceptance do not certify arbitrary nnn. A size-dependent physical frontier requires validated phase/approximation and postselection-sensitive synthesis allowances, a declared output observable and shot budget, and physical-fault allocation across every attempt. That frontier remains pending. The in-page dynamics resource contract records these boundaries.

Verification and formalization¶

Actual circuit blocks are compared with independent spectral cosine and matrix-exponential references, while scalar response, coefficient reconstruction and truncation evidence are recorded separately. Additional negative/Y coefficients, complex inputs and nonnormal amplification tests check phase and conditioning behavior. These are bounded numerical checks. No reproduced Lean theorem or certified synthesis sequence is included.
