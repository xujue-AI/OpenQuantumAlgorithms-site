Qubitization and quantum signal processing

Quantum signal processing (QSP) turns repeated access to a normalized Hamiltonian into a polynomial of that Hamiltonian. The Hamiltonian dynamics task fixes the three-spin open chain, t=1t=1t=1, input ∣000⟩|000\rangle∣000⟩ and observable Z0Z_0Z0​. This method page keeps its algorithm, numerical verification and complete logical costs together.

The retargetable QSP variant generates phases for changed sizes and times, with fixed local couplings and a requested accuracy. It includes an interval coefficient validator and a separate logical loading-error bound. The frozen αt=4.1\alpha t=4.1αt=4.1 sequence on this page remains a checked reference case.

Implementation status

Actual PREPARE/SELECT/unprepare access, zero-label reflections, real QSP filtering and a cosine/sine selector circuit are implemented. Frozen phases and small gate circuits are checked against independent scalar and dense classical answers. Integer-round oblivious amplification is a separately checked variant. The computed bounded study supplies conditional surface-code projections for fixed-coupling complex QSP and its three-spin amplification cases. The retargetable variant supplies bounded phase generation and logical-source interval contracts. Deterministic evolution, unsupported higher-degree regimes and certified hardware synthesis remain planned.

Motivation and the fundamental version¶

A product formula approximates time evolution by short local rotations. QSP instead treats a Hamiltonian eigenvalue as a signal: for H∣v⟩=E∣v⟩H|v\rangle=E|v\rangleH∣v⟩=E∣v⟩, let x=E/αx=E/\alphax=E/α and α=∑j∣hj∣\alpha=\sum_j|h_j|α=∑j​∣hj​∣. Since ∣x∣≤1|x|\le1∣x∣≤1, the access circuit and a zero-label reflection preserve a two-dimensional subspace with signal matrix

Bx=(x1−x21−x2−x).B_x=\begin{pmatrix}x&\sqrt{1-x^2}\\\sqrt{1-x^2}&-x\end{pmatrix}.Bx​=(x1−x2​​1−x2​−x​).
(1)

The qubitization walk (2Π−I)B(2\Pi-I)B(2Π−I)B rotates this subspace through eigenphases ±arccos⁡x\pm\arccos x±arccosx. Phase rotations interleaved with access calls interfere so that the accepted component becomes a chosen bounded polynomial in xxx, applied to every eigencomponent without computing eigenvectors Low & Chuang, 2019Gilyén et al., 2019.

Here Π=∣0⟩⟨0∣label⊗Isystem\Pi=|0\rangle\langle0|_{\rm label}\otimes I_{\rm system}Π=∣0⟩⟨0∣label​⊗Isystem​ and

D(ϕ)=eiϕ(2Π−I),V=D(ϕ0)BD(ϕ1)⋯BD(ϕd).D(\phi)=e^{i\phi(2\Pi-I)},\qquad V=D(\phi_0)B D(\phi_1)\cdots B D(\phi_d).D(ϕ)=eiϕ(2Π−I),V=D(ϕ0​)BD(ϕ1​)⋯BD(ϕd​).
(2)

The scalar implementation multiplies matrices in this written order. Circuit gates are applied chronologically in reverse phase order, beginning with ϕd\phi_dϕd​. This convention matters when importing phases. A flag in ∣+⟩|+\rangle∣+⟩ selects opposite phase signs, then H and flag-zero acceptance extract the real part. The implemented signed phase is eiϕ(2Π−I)Zfe^{i\phi(2\Pi-I)Z_f}eiϕ(2Π−I)Zf​: an RZ(2ϕ)R_Z(2\phi)RZ​(2ϕ) on the flag and a zero-label-controlled RZ(−4ϕ)R_Z(-4\phi)RZ​(−4ϕ) supply both contributions. Ignoring a branch phase changes the polynomial.

A degree-ddd real polynomial needs ddd complete block calls and d+1d+1d+1 projector phases. Parity restricts a single real transform: even cosine and odd sine require separate sequences. For the ideal block-access model, Hamiltonian-simulation degree can scale as O(α∣t∣+log⁡(1/ϵ)/log⁡log⁡(1/ϵ))O(\alpha|t|+\log(1/\epsilon)/\log\log(1/\epsilon))O(α∣t∣+log(1/ϵ)/loglog(1/ϵ)) in the small-error regime Low & Chuang, 2017Low & Chuang, 2019. This is a query bound with specified access; our direct gate construction must also pay coefficient loading, SELECT, controls, uncomputation, synthesis and measured repetitions. Frozen numerical phases below cover one αt\alpha tαt and do not implement a general optimal-degree phase compiler.

Planned generalized QSP variant¶

Generalized QSP uses general ancilla SU(2) rotations to construct a complex polynomial P(W)P(W)P(W) of a unitary signal WWW, with ∣P(z)∣≤1|P(z)|\leq1∣P(z)∣≤1 on the complex unit circle Motlagh & Wiebe, 2024. Its signal convention removes the fixed polynomial-parity constraint and can change the cosine/sine combination used for Hamiltonian simulation. Completing a complementary polynomial and constructing the rotations are part of the algorithm.

The existing Hermitian BxB_xBx​ sequence, fitted phases and accepted block near U/4U/4U/4 retain their present evidence and retry contract. A GQSP companion is planned: it needs its own unitary signal/access circuit, convention check, rotation generator, independent small polynomial and evolution checks, acceptance analysis and full synthesis/preparation/readout costs. Qualtran’s inspected HamiltonianSimulationByGQSP is a prospective implementation source Qualtran contributors, 2026; its compositional model has not been adopted or reproduced here.

Explicit block access and cosine filtering¶

The five Hamiltonian terms have α=∑j∣cj∣=4.1\alpha=\sum_j|c_j|=4.1α=∑j​∣cj​∣=4.1. Explicit PREPARE and signed Pauli SELECT give the Hermitian involution

PREPARE∣0⟩=∑j∣hj∣/α ∣j⟩,SELECT=∑j∣j⟩⟨j∣⊗sgn⁡(hj)Pj.\mathrm{PREPARE}|0\rangle=\sum_j\sqrt{|h_j|/\alpha}\,|j\rangle,\qquad \mathrm{SELECT}=\sum_j|j\rangle\langle j|\otimes\operatorname{sgn}(h_j)P_j.PREPARE∣0⟩=j∑​∣hj​∣/α​∣j⟩,SELECT=j∑​∣j⟩⟨j∣⊗sgn(hj​)Pj​.
(3)

The loader is a tree of controlled RY gates. SELECT applies indexed Pauli gates and coefficient signs, and unused binary labels apply identity. For real coefficients each selected operation squares to identity. The complete access is

B=PREPARE†SELECT PREPARE,⟨0∣B∣0⟩=H/α.B=\mathrm{PREPARE}^\dagger\mathrm{SELECT}\,\mathrm{PREPARE},\qquad\langle0|B|0\rangle=H/\alpha.B=PREPARE†SELECTPREPARE,⟨0∣B∣0⟩=H/α.
(4)

Tests check the actual block. A reflection about the zero-label subspace creates the qubitization walk and its invariant-subspace identity is checked Low & Chuang, 2019.

Complex QSP dynamics with explicit postselection¶

The next circuit completes the cosine/sine combination for the fixed dynamics task. Freeze an even degree-14 phase sequence for C(x)≈cos⁡(4.1x)/2C(x)\approx\cos(4.1x)/2C(x)≈cos(4.1x)/2 and an odd degree-15 sequence for S(x)≈sin⁡(4.1x)/2S(x)\approx\sin(4.1x)/2S(x)≈sin(4.1x)/2. A uniform selector and a relative −i-i−i phase on its sine branch give the zero-selector block

K(x)=C(x)−iS(x)2≈e−i4.1x4.K(x)=\frac{C(x)-iS(x)}{2}\approx\frac{e^{-i4.1x}}{4}.K(x)=2C(x)−iS(x)​≈4e−i4.1x​.
(5)

This is the QSP/LCU normalization, not a deterministic unitary on the system Low & Chuang, 2017Gilyén et al., 2019Martyn et al., 2021. The generator shares fourteen ordinary BBB calls between the branches, adds one actual selector-controlled PREPARE/SELECT/inverse-PREPARE call, and implements every selector-conditioned projector phase. It does not append a matrix exponential gate. All three label bits, the real-part flag and the selector must be zero to accept; all relative branch phases are included in the circuit and in decomposition checks.

If ppp is the numerical acceptance lower bound and the synthesis-model scalar ϵ\epsilonϵ bounds total variation of the complete final joint data/ancilla law, then acceptance is at least p−ϵp-\epsilonp−ϵ and conditional Pauli bias is at most 2ϵ/p2\epsilon/p2ϵ/p. The allocation uses ϵ=.01p/2\epsilon=.01p/2ϵ=.01p/2. The native mean fit does not establish this distributional assumption.

Each accepted slot receives the smallest integer retry cap whose no-acceptance union bound is at most .001 across all 13,811 slots. The unamplified two-, three- and four-spin cases require 257 complete attempts per slot, or 3,549,427 capped complete attempts. Every source/inverse access, reflection, rejected schedule and final data/ancilla readout is charged. The .005 physical-fault allowance is divided across the maximum complete attempts. Reports give no certified output-confidence bound and no simultaneous success guarantee across the sweep.

All methods in the dynamics study keep the same physical profiles: error 10-3 or 10-4, 100 ns gate time and 500 ns measurement time. The frozen search domain contains every odd data and factory distance from 3 through 41. It was extended for all dynamics methods because the native 3–25 domain could not accommodate the complete unamplified-QSP 10-3 workload. Accuracy, fault and retry budgets were not relaxed. Grover’s separate frozen search and artifacts remain unchanged.

The plots select the fewest-qubits point and that point’s capped serial runtime. Peak qubits times capped runtime is a rectangular spacetime proxy, not integrated occupancy, and the finite search is not a global optimality certificate. At 10-3 the three-spin round-zero point uses 1,040,665 peak qubits and 5,667,238.762 capped seconds; round two uses the same peak qubits and 880,139.784 seconds, while round three costs 924,002.719 seconds. An additional amplification round therefore need not reduce the complete workload. The product-formula resource slices use the same observable contract and hardware assumptions; the in-page dynamics analysis explains the comparison and its limitations. These costs supply no general QSP advantage claim.

Verification and formal evidence¶

Verification proceeds through independent scalar signal matrices, frozen-phase residuals, exact small Hamiltonians, the accepted gate-circuit block, complex conditional states and measured observables. Tests check Y terms, negative coefficients, branch-relative phases and primitive decomposition. The analytic Bessel tail bounds polynomial truncation; floating coefficient reconstruction and a sampled grid remain numerical evidence. Synthesis must supply an actual coherent norm guarantee before it can be combined with postselection-sensitive state and acceptance bounds.

The static resource study additionally checks all measured QASM hashes against the costed circuits, complete readout/retry counts, synthesis allocations and modeled physical union bounds. Its retargeted basis trajectories and analytic phase-Jacobian checks extend the earlier normalized-sweep evidence; they do not convert numerical phase fitting or compiler equivalence into a theorem.

The current Python package includes optional phase recomputation with fit_retargeted_phases. A real degree-14 even or degree-15 odd polynomial has eight parity coefficients, so each continuation step uses a fixed eight-dimensional subspace of the Jacobian at its starting phases. The fitter retains a maximum step of 0.2, 500 joint response/Jacobian evaluations per step and a 60-second deadline per component; acceptance requires a maximum node residual ≤10−8\le10^{-8}≤10−8. Checks compare the fitted response with the stored sequences and independent signal matrices, including rotations of the fitting coordinates. The resource circuits use the recorded phase sequences above.

No QSP-specific Lean source, locked proof toolchain or correctness artifact is integrated. QSP Lean coverage is planned. A future proof of a signal identity or individual gate would have to state its scope separately from full phase synthesis, approximation, retries and physical correctness. The current evidence is executable numerical verification.
